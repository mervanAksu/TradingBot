### 002 | 20220122 | Elements of a trade setup (first teaching: weekly bias -> liquidity run -> MSS -> FVG entry)

- RULE: FVG = three candles; the gap between candle 1's low and candle 3's high (bearish case) after the breaking candle; entry = limit sell as price re-enters the gap, or drop one TF lower (3m -> 1m) to find a finer gap inside the same area (@29:03, @30:04, @32:09)
- RULE: Stop = above the gap-forming candle's high or above the raid high, whichever fits risk; alternative sell-stop entry below the swing low accepted but gives a wider stop (@32:40, @43:57)
- RULE: Short-term swing low (for MSS) = candle whose low is below the lows of the candles on each side (3-bar); MSS = trade below it (@43:26)
- RULE: Do not chase after the low is already taken - market order after the break = slippage and larger risk; the entry belongs on the retrace up into the gap (@33:41, @34:12)
- RULE: Target = nearest opposing liquidity/imbalance below the 50% of the range (take the "low hanging fruit"), not the farthest low (@47:34, @48:04)
- DEF: Premium/discount via 50% of a Fib dragged across the day's range so far; above 50 = premium (sell zone), below = discount. Short entry should sit in premium and aim below the 50 (@35:13, @36:14)
- DEF: Draw on liquidity: price is pulled either to resting stops (above old highs = buy stops, below old lows = sell stops) or to an imbalance (single-direction candle sequence with no opposite delivery) (@17:16, @17:46)
- DEF: Imbalance resolves when price later trades back through it with the opposite-direction candle; the target gap near the low here was "closed" by that move (@38:17, @38:47)
