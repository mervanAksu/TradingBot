### 042 | 20241004 | October NFP NQ
tags: news-event protocol, minor/primary buy/sell side liquidity (L4), opening range gap (ORG), CE/mid-gap, FVG/IFVG, volume imbalance, Asia macro, Judas swing
- RULE: NFP (and other tier-1 news) = no-trade window: no positions ahead of 8:30; after release wait >= 15 min (first tradable look 8:45 NY), then trade normally the rest of the day. Never chase the first candle (slippage) (@05:47, @10:53, @17:06)
- RULE: Pre-news liquidity map on 15m at ~8:15: mark nearest swing high = minor BSL, nearest swing low = minor SSL; then the next logical pool beyond = primary (relative equal highs / lows past the consolidation, ignoring the small consolidation lows). Whichever side the news spike takes first is observed, then look to the opposing pool (@13:28, @18:38, @21:24)
- RULE: Lows/highs that are already exceeded by later extremes do not count as pools (only the lowest of a stack of lows is the pool) (@29:22)
- DEF: Largest resting liquidity is always beyond old highs/lows (buy stops above highs, sell stops below lows). A void/imbalance (sell-side-delivered, not yet repriced) is only a delivery target, not where the big orders are; price may fill it and keep going to the real stop pool (@53:12, @54:15)
- DEF: "Sell-side imbalance / buy-side inefficiency" = range printed by one-direction delivery; expected re-delivery from the opposite side over the same price span and, in time, similar speed/size. Efficient delivery just means it has been repriced through, not that it is a BPR (@55:52, @60:32)
- RULE: Sputtering / back-and-forth movement while repricing through an inefficiency = high-resistance, manipulated; do not trail; just take cover-cost partials (@61:34)
- RULE: IFVG use: if price runs into the news FVG, trades through it and returns to it from the other side, treat it as an inverse FVG for the continuation to the minor opposing pool (@32:17, @41:09)
- CTX: NFP disrupts the currently profitable side (here Tuesday's shorts); expect run of their stops (relative equal highs = primary BSL) rather than just a fill of the inefficiency (@57:27, @63:37)
- DEF: Opening Range Gap (ORG) = RTH prior-day settlement (16:15) to 9:30 open, always defined on RTH even if electronic chart is shown; mid-gap = 50% = CE of it. Stat claimed: ~70% likelihood to trade to mid-gap within first 30 min (9:30-10:00) (@66:12, @70:18, @85:19)
- CTX: Large premium ORG expected: possible Judas-type run up at the open to sweep ETH highs (buy stops), then sell back to mid-gap / 3/4 / full close and beyond (2-3 standard deviations of ORG projection) (@67:46, @73:55)
- CTX: Standard day (small gap): wait for the first FVG after 9:30 and trade toward mid-gap, then decide continuation vs full closure (@72:21, @73:25)
- RULE: Asia long protocol: no medium/high news between 8:30-10:00 next NY day; in the last RTH hour price must sweep an old low/EQL and stay there into the close; then buy around the 18:50-19:10 macro (a late start at 19:50-20:10 macro gives ~20-40 pts); work Asia 19:00-21:00, square or leave runner at 22:00. If the last hour rallies from a low instead, probability drops (@77:00, @79:04, @81:09, @83:45)
- DEF: Asia can build the low (bull) / high (bear) of the day; London then often consolidates and NY repeats the move; 700 pts from 18:00 open to NY high is possible but not typical (@80:09, @83:15)
- RULE: FVG with volume imbalance inside: draw the zone from body-gap prices (candle 2 close to candle 3 open when candle1/2 bodies touch), not wick-to-wick; zone then quartered - bodies closing in the upper quadrant vs staying outside it reveal bias (bearish = bodies refuse upper half) (@117:24, @118:24)
- EX: Oct 4 2024 NQ 1m/5m: 8:30 candle ~98 pts range; ran minor BSL then continued up; after 9:30 price failed at upper quadrant of the ORG-side FVG, displaced down with an FVG (breakaway gap) and hit the ORG mid-gap before 10:00 (@26:46, @109:45, @119:24)
- EX: Same day: 'volatility pinball' drill = trade every FVG to the nearby high/low, many reps, as conditioning only (@45:44)

### 043 | 20241007 | NQ High Resistance Liquidity Conditions Tape Reading
tags: LRLR/HRLR conditions (L5/L6), opening range gap, first-presented FVG, IFVG, volume imbalance, NWOG, mid-gap
- DEF: Condition test (run on the 15m after the first 30 min): if price keeps spending many bars inside FVGs / imbalances and makes deep pullbacks after every push, the market is in a high-resistance liquidity run (HRLR); clean one-way displacements that reach targets almost without retracing = low-resistance (LRLR). Condition is a property of the delivery, judged from behaviour, not from the target (@51:04, @56:53)
- RULE: In HRLR conditions expect imprecise PD-array delivery: reactions are deferred, retracements deeper, and price often does NOT return to the first-presented FVG; so sit out the morning, skip trades, and expect cleaner setups in lunch/PM only if the AM was messy (@40:25, @60:21)
- RULE: Opening-range bias recipe (9:30-10:00): bias = draw to mid-gap (50% of ORG). Entry = price trades above the first-presented 1m FVG (formed 9:31-10:00), returns to it, treats it as an inverse FVG, displaces up and prints a new FVG on 15s; enter on that subsequent FVG, not on the touch. Price merely sitting in the gap then moving away is not the entry (@19:41, @21:23, @24:26, @60:51)
- RULE: If no qualifying FVG forms in the 30 min window, there is no trade; after 10:15 stop for the morning (@37:34, @39:54)
- RULE: Short mirror for PM: after trading above the gap/mid-gap or NWOG, a break below the first FVG, a return up into it, then displacement down with a 15s FVG = entry (@54:45)
- RULE: Volume imbalance is a soft level: price may trade through it and back; the defining price is the body/low of the candle (one tick variance was enough) (@59:20)
- DEF: Previous Friday settlement = RTH gap reference; NWOG low CE sits above price as the next draw beyond mid-gap (@54:13)
- CTX: Day-of context: no news Mon/Tue, CPI/PPI later in week; Monday after a weekend: be slow to act (@02:39, @20:13)
- EX: Oct 7 2024 NQ 1m/15s: ORG high ~22222.5-area, mid-gap ~21795; early 1m FVG, price dipped to take minor SSL, no valid inversion setup; later price swept below a 5m volume imbalance by one tick and rallied to mid-gap (@10:33, @59:51)

### 044 | 20241015 | NQ Tape Reading (overnight range grading)
tags: overnight range (L8-like), premium/discount grading (S3), FVG qualification (P1), volume imbalance (P4), macros, session H/L (L3), ORG, daily SIBI, CE, protraction vs consolidation
- RULE: Overnight range (ONR) = highest high and lowest low of bars between 00:01 and 04:59 NY (exclude the 00:00 and 05:00 candles; chart TZ must be NY). Grade it with a fib: 0 / 0.25 / 0.5 (equilibrium) / 0.75 / 1 (@08:48, @09:49, @10:50, @16:30)
- RULE: ONR applies only when overnight stayed inside the prior day's range (part of a larger consolidation, no one-way run from Asia). If Asia-London was a one-sided protraction/trend, do not grade it; treat the NY AM as likely reversal-or-consolidation, so prefer London-lunch/PM/last-hour trades (@11:52, @51:03, @88:38)
- RULE: FVG qualification (bullish bias): only take FVGs located below ONR equilibrium (discount) and near it; bearish bias mirrors above EQ. Upper half of ONR = lower precision (price tends to just run/leave), so do not expect deep returns there (@23:10, @26:20, @31:27, @60:18)
- RULE: Pre-market (06:00-09:30) macro windows: 05:50-06:10, 06:50-07:10, 07:50-08:10, 08:50-09:10 plus the 08:30 algo = ~5 setups; every hour has a macro from :50 to :10 (20-min window) (@37:19, @52:03)
- RULE: Targets via ONR: partials at ONR EQ, upper quadrant, ONR high, previous-day high, daily SIBI levels ("horseshoe/ringer" partial laddering); extension projections off the ONR range: -0.5 first, -1 = one standard deviation (@59:47, @65:27)
- RULE: Entry at HTF levels is one tick beyond: price can trade 1 tick through the CE of a daily FVG/SIBI before reversing (target the CE minus/plus 1 tick) (@19:36)
- DEF: Daily single down-close candle (Jul 17 2024) is treated as a sell-side imbalance / buy-side inefficiency (SIBI) = a daily FVG-like range: candle1 low to candle3 high (the gap between the two outer candles); quadrants (0.25/0.5/0.75) of it act as reaction levels during higher-TF bullish bias (@03:49, @66:08)
- DEF: Opening-gap size threshold: ORG < ~45-50 handles (NQ) = anemic, no morning edge, AM is 50/50: sit still until 10:00-11:00 Silver Bullet or defer to lunch macro / PM / last hour. ORG >= ~75 handles = trade at the open toward 50% (mid-gap) with 70% hit within 30 min, no need to wait for the FVG (@39:54, @40:25, @42:59)
- RULE: PM session liquidity: record highs/lows made in 13:30-14:00 on each of last 3 days (PM-session open range) plus PM session H/L; these are recurring liquidity references (@46:21)
- RULE: Daily levels: know last 5 daily highs/lows and last week's H/L (and Friday); after price runs above an old high and the return does not reach the equal lows below (the 'bait'), expect a protraction in bias direction; enter on the inversion FVG formed on the pullback, do not wait for a touch of the old high (S/R style) (@69:43, @72:48)
- RULE: Entry execution: use sub-1m chart (30s; 15s too noisy); enter while price is travelling against the trade into the FVG / volume imbalance (candle opening high, trading down into zone for longs); volume imbalance bodies "respecting" = confirmation (@79:26, @81:31)
- MMXM: Roles invert: every bullish FVG/OB used in a buy bias becomes IFVG / bearish OB in a market maker sell model (mirror, same logic on the other side) (@64:23)
- CTX: 08:30 algo spools price every day irrespective of news; with a news driver the move is exaggerated and not necessarily directional (@29:53)
- CTX: Order flow window theory: big entities feed orders through dealers in the :50-:10 macros to get average price; macros are time windows of liquidity delivery, not price causers (@52:34, @54:40)
- EX: Oct 15 2024 NQ 1m: ONR formed in a consolidation; low at 05:08 went 1 tick below CE of the Jul-17 daily SIBI (20568 vs low 20567.75) at Asia close, rallied; FVG at 05:50-06:10 macro below ONR EQ delivered to EQ; later FVG again below EQ delivered to the upper quadrant and Monday's high (@18:02, @22:39, @24:16)
- EX: Oct 14 2024 NQ 30s: Oct 10 high taken, pullback made an IFVG + volume imbalance; long at 08:30 macro to 20538 objective; PM short to sell side before holiday-shortened close (@72:48, @84:02, @86:07)

### 045 | 20241016 | How To Navigate Decoupled Markets
tags: SMT divergence (L9), decoupling NQ/ES/YM, first-presented FVG, IFVG, order block (change in state of delivery), ORG, volume imbalance, MMBM
- DEF: Decoupling = the three US index averages (NQ, ES, YM) trending in different directions during the AM with no news; it lowers precision/visibility of PD arrays. Dow is the least important; its lower-low/higher-low mismatch is NOT counted as SMT; the SMT that counts is NQ vs ES (@06:44, @07:15, @67:18)
- RULE: Filter: only trade when all three averages move in the same direction as your instrument; if decoupled, either wait for them to re-sync (may take the AM; PM often better) or stand aside; the day can stay decoupled all session (@07:45, @12:02, @29:09)
- RULE: Forecast pattern (decoupled AM): after NQ takes its sell side (lower low) while ES refuses to make a lower low (ES "shrugs off weakness") = SMT; NQ then returns into its range / opening gap and to the first-presented FVG; first FVG does not need to be retraced to before the sell-side run (@19:17, @37:56, @55:56, @65:16)
- RULE: IFVG entry: when bias is strongly directional, enter inside the inverse FVG (at its edge or at its CE / mean threshold) as the candle that closes through it prints, rather than waiting for a retest; the retest may never come. No rule is given to know when it won't (acknowledged as discretionary) (@39:59, @43:03, @45:09)
- RULE: Staying-open condition: a new FVG left unfilled behind a move is a breakaway gap; price should not return to it, so do not wait for a retest (@49:48, @60:09)
- DEF: Order-block variant for the AM: two consecutive up-closed candles; the open of the first (lowest) up-close candle is the "change in state of delivery" level; price returned there and reversed, low to the tick (low = candle low + 1 tick) (@22:59, @23:29)
- DEF: Body-vs-wick rule: bodies laying/stopping at CE or beyond the half of an FVG/wick, wicks allowed to overshoot; bodies show respect (@14:32, @22:28)
- DEF: Delivery without displacement = overlapping candles revisiting the same prices ("fuzzy"): signature of no-edge conditions; clean sequences of separated ranges (FVG/VI one after another) = tradable (@68:22)
- MMXM: A buy model need not return all the way to the original consolidation - the retracement to the first FVG / volume imbalance can replace a full return to the original consolidation; but you must know the draw (@69:54)
- CTX: ES = slower, NQ more volatile; ICT prefers NQ; ES in a mess when decoupled (@26:33)
- CTX: Small/anemic opening gap on this day again; first FVG forms in same area as the ORG (gap share same real estate) (@18:41, @20:48)

## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules:
  1. News (NFP/CPI/FOMC) day: no entries before release; wait >=15 min; map nearest minor BSL/SSL at 8:15 and next primary pools, then trade toward the opposite pool after the first one is taken (042)
  2. ORG = RTH 16:14 settle to 9:30 print; mid-gap (50%) reached in first 30 min about 70%; discount gap -> long bias until 50%, premium gap -> short bias; gap >= ~75 handles trade the open directly, < ~45-50 = sit out AM (042, 044, 047)
  3. Overnight range = 00:01-04:59 NY high/low, graded at 0.25/0.5/0.75; in a bullish bias only take FVGs below EQ (discount), mirror for bearish; not valid if overnight was a one-way protraction (044)
  4. Macro windows :50-:10 each hour (pre-market 05:50, 06:50, 07:50, 08:50 plus 8:30 algo) as entry times (044)
  5. First-presented FVG (9:31-10:00, 1m) + IFVG on 15s/30s: enter on the subsequent FVG after price trades through and returns; or enter inside the IFVG at CE (043, 045)
  6. HRLR vs LRLR: judge by the bar behaviour (long time inside FVG/VI, deep pullbacks) -> in HRLR expect no precision, trade less (043)
  7. Decoupling filter: NQ/ES/YM must agree; NQ lower low with ES failing = SMT and signals return to range; ignore Dow divergence (045)
  8. Volume imbalance rules: soft array, draw from lowest body of adjacent indecisive candles; becomes a BPR/support after both up-body and down-body laid over it (046)
  9. Asia long protocol: no AM news next day + last hour sweeps a low and holds -> buy 18:50-19:10 or 19:50-20:10 macro; 19:00-21:00 work window (042)
  10. Daily/session levels: last 5 daily H/L, previous week H/L, 13:30-14:00 PM-open range, session H/L of Asia/London/AM/PM as liquidity references (044, 047)
- Definition changes vs DRAFT: ORG uses RTH 16:14 settle (not 17:00 close) and is defined on RTH only; NWOG = ETH settle to Sunday open (separate from ORG); LRLR/HRLR defined by delivery behaviour, not by PD arrays between price and pool; FVG/VI edge drawn on bodies when volume imbalance present; SMT only NQ vs ES (Dow excluded); largest liquidity always above highs/below lows, never in imbalances; IFVG/entry allowed inside the gap at CE before retest.
- New concepts not in key-levels.md: Overnight range grading (00:01-04:59); first-presented FVG (9:31-10:00); change in state of delivery (open of first up-close candle in a 2-candle series, used as an OB); breakaway gap; decoupled-index filter; macro windows (:50-:10); gap-quadrant bodies test; 30s chart execution; mid-gap/70% stat; horseshoe/ringer partials; sell-side imbalance/buy-side inefficiency (SIBI) daily range; PM-open (13:30-14:00) liquidity; volatility pinball drill; propulsion block re-entry.
- MMXM explicit material in this batch: very thin. Only (a) 045@69:54 "MM buy model need not clear the original consolidation" (retracement to FVG/VI can suffice), (b) 044@64:23 bullish arrays mirror into a "market maker sell model" (IFVG/bearish OB), (c) 046@13:57 pre-news consolidation "resembles first stage accumulation". No SMR, no 2nd stage, no low-risk definitions, no buy/sell-side curve.
