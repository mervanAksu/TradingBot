### 048 | 20241025 | How ICT Picks Winning FVGs & Order Blocks (grading the dealing range)
tags: large-range-day, inside day, LRLR/HRLR, dealing range quadrants, premium/discount (S3), FVG (P1), order block (P6), mean threshold, consequent encroachment, NWOG (P5), macros (T5), power of 3 (T2), liquidity draw (L4)
- RULE: Large-range-day (LRD) candidate filter = today's daily range smaller than the average daily range of the last 3 days (a "small-range / inside day" = high below prior high AND low above prior low). Compression is a precursor; the expansion comes next day, 2nd day at worst. A 5-day average is a more sensitive alternative; 7-day narrowest range is the borrowed (Crabel) variant (@07:33, @09:38, @97:57, @98:30)
- RULE: Compression gives no direction. Direction = draw on liquidity: after a sell-off day, look at where smooth (relative equal) highs were left resting above; retail shorts defend them. Bias = toward the smooth side, ignore shorting (@11:41, @19:28, @29:17)
- RULE: On a bullish LRD the day/session open (midnight NY open; for the AM session the 9:30 open) sits in the low part of the daily range (~lower quarter); buy below the open after a manipulation drop, then accumulate around the open, then expand (Power-of-3 read) (@21:34, @23:36, @27:43)
- RULE: ~90% of large-range days deliver as low-resistance liquidity run (LRLR): little overlap, down-closed candles/bullish OBs hold as support, FVGs stay partly open; ~90% of non-large days are high-resistance (HRLR): overlapping bars, FVGs get closed through, price scribbles. Use as a regime classifier (@79:27, @98:30, @99:01)
- RULE: Avoid the AM session on the day after a large-range day (precision evades; HRLR "fuzzy" action); a small inside day is also choppy intraday (@07:02, @43:24, @45:27)
- RULE (core, "grading a swing"): define the dealing range as the swing high of the current retracement/run down to the discount low (use candle bodies, ignore wick stop-runs); EQ = 50%; split into quadrants (25/75 lines). Long entries only at or below EQ; pyramiding/adds limited to EQ or lower; the best, lowest-risk entries are at/below the lower quadrant. An FVG/OB is a valid candidate only if the bulk of it sits at or below the lower quadrant (for longs) - a gap whose body is mostly above that line is skipped (@67:05, @68:08, @71:44, @77:56)
- RULE: Sequence for a long: bullish bias -> range defined -> price trades into a discount array at/below the lower quadrant -> wait for displacement that breaks the nearest short-term high (a swing high = candle with lower highs both sides) = MSS, leaving FVG/volume imbalance -> enter on a return to the array. Do not buy merely because price touches the zone (@70:42, @71:14)
- DEF: Order block (bullish) here = the down-closed candle (or run of 2+ consecutive down-closed candles) with the lowest body, bodies only, wick ignored. Its 50% of the body = mean threshold (MT); "consequent encroachment" (CE) is reserved for gaps/inefficiencies/wicks. Demo: price reversed exactly at MT of the OB and next bar's low printed on that same price (@73:50, @74:50)
- DEF: "Institutional order-flow entry drill" = entry model for an FVG that price is expected to trade into but not fully close: limit at the upper-quadrant/CE level (e.g. quadrant price +1 tick), stop just beyond the candle holding the gap's high; valid only in LRLR conditions. Can also be a gap whose CE overlaps the range EQ (double midpoint confluence) - expect only a partial fill, lower part of the gap stays open (breakaway gap) (@80:28, @81:29, @82:33, @84:35)
- RULE: Entry preference when bullish and in LRLR: the very lowest candle of the pullback or one candle either side; do not enter at the extreme low of an OB candle, want MT or higher up to its high (@87:42, @88:14)
- RULE: In LRLR do not rush partials and do not trail the stop tightly to each swing; set a reasonable stop, place limit targets, walk away. Trailing in HRLR gets stopped, trailing in LRLR is premature (@99:32, @102:36, @103:06)
- RULE: Expecting a LRD with a big opening gap: do not expect the NWOG/opening gap to be mean-filled in the first 30 min (normal ~70% half-gap fill is overridden); enter pre-market (about 07:00-09:00) and hold, partial along the way, pyramid on the lunch retracement if the 10:00 hour allows (@16:17, @29:47, @59:22, @60:24)
- MMXM: Pre-market micro-sequence shown: 8:30 macro spools price into a discount array -> displacement through short-term highs (MSS) -> retrace to bullish OB/FVG in discount -> run to buy-side. Model 2022 style: MSS + FVG, retrace to premium/OTE for shorts is the mirror. If nothing spools at 8:30, wait for the 9:30 open (@70:42, @72:49, @92:20)
- RULE (time): macros = first 10 min before and after every top of hour; 8:30 is the only half-hour one; last-hour macro 15:15-15:45; lunch window 11:30-13:30 (lunch macro takes the profitable side's stops and defines the range of that window); 10:30-11:30 usually forms the AM higher low (@31:17, @31:49, @72:16, @94:54)
- RULE: Split news (8:30 and 10:00) -> first release is a fake run, expect whipsaw; wait for the 10:00 data before real run (@28:46)
- CTX: Last trading day of week with unfinished buy-side: price rushes to the liquidity at the open (Friday must finish before 17:00; no weekend gap risk wanted); sudden one-way run "looks like manual intervention" (@20:32, @101:03)
- CTX: Only a handful of liquidity references are needed: session/PM/lunch/pre-market/London/Asia highs and lows, not every NDOG/NWOG; the pool may be a single smooth high not just equal highs (@89:44, @94:54)
- CTX: Risk note: into US election week lower size (1 contract/micros) - non-mechanical (@106:14)
- EX: Fri 25 Oct 2024 NQ 15m/1m: Thu inside day after Wed sell-off; Fri opened near low, dropped to discount, bullish OB MT = low of next candle at 20452.75, then ran to the smooth highs / NWOG CE area ~20495 and beyond; FVG-1 skipped (above lower quadrant), FVG-2 used (@73:50, @89:14)
- skip: Long rants (copyright, mentorship, personal losses of $2k on Thu, election, trading psychology). Only the grading-of-swing and LRLR material is operational.

### 049 | 20241028 | High Resistance & Low Resistance Conditions (Mon, no news; pre-market trading; "Caleb" model)
tags: LRLR/HRLR (L5/L6), NWOG/NDOG clustering (P5), opening range gap (ORG) 70% rule, news/calendar filter, pre-market window, FVG stop placement (P1), reclaimed PD arrays, macros (T5)
- DEF (core): HRLR vs LRLR is classified by candle overlap. HRLR = each new candle overlaps most (>= ~half) of the previous candle's range, scribbling, deep retracements, FVGs/OBs get re-visited ("reclaimed" FVG/OB = same array touched twice is a signature of HRLR), FVGs close in. LRLR = large separated candles, many left-open inefficiencies, little re-touching, price moves in a hurry to the target (@113:55, @114:56, @121:35, @122:36)
- RULE: Calendar/day-type proxy: a medium/high-impact news driver at 8:30 or inside the first AM hour on a non-Monday = the climate where LRLR happens (manipulation first traps retail, then fast one-way move). Not a guarantee. No 8:30 / 9:30-10:30 news (esp. a Monday) = expect HRLR in the 9:30 AM session (@126:43, @128:14, @128:44, @130:17)
- RULE: No-news Monday -> do not trade the 9:30 AM session; trade the pre-market window 07:00-09:00 NY only (get in/out before 9:30), because 9:30 delivers back-and-forth against the unprepared. Monday is not banned, just restricted (@06:59, @08:36, @12:12, @14:45)
- RULE: NFP week: trade Monday and Tuesday (cleanest), finish by Wednesday AM; Thursday/Fri only if very obvious (@10:38, @11:08, @11:40)
- RULE: Draw proxy ("low-hanging objective"): use clustered unfilled NWOG (or NDOG) as the draw: take the last ~5 NDOGs/NWOGs; any two whose ranges overlap = cluster; strongest magnet = open (unfilled) gaps. Short bias: first target = HIGHEST level of the cluster nearest price (the NWOG high), exit when it trades there +1-2 ticks; long mirror uses lowest level. Do not hold for CE/low of the gaps (@24:28, @38:25, @39:25, @67:28, @111:55)
- RULE: Opening range gap (9:30 RTH open vs prior 16:00 settlement): ~70% probability price trades to the half-gap (CE of ORG) within the first 30 min; when a clustered NWOG/NDOG lies beyond it in the same direction, expect the half-gap hit first then continuation toward the cluster. Opening-range scripts are 30 min and 60 min (no "15-min opening range"); first 30/60 min tends to define the daily high/low zone (@74:10, @78:34, @93:22, @104:45, @105:15)
- RULE: Beginner entry model ("Caleb"): 15s-1m chart; HTF array = one-candle 1m FVG/sell-side imbalance (single-candle displacement); wait for price to react off it (bodies respect it) and displace, then enter on the first clean FVG after 9:31 (not on the 9:30 candle's gap); entry = limit at the low end of the gap for shorts (one tick beyond), never CE or high of the gap; 1 contract in HRLR (@94:58, @95:29, @99:09, @107:52)
- RULE: FVG stop rule: bullish/bearish FVG consists of candle 1, candle 2 (middle, the displacement), candle 3. Low-risk stop = high of candle 2 (+1 tick) for a short in LRLR; if the FVG sits too close to the opposing HTF array or in HRLR conditions use the wider stop beyond candle 1's high (+1 tick) (@100:42, @101:12, @101:42)
- RULE: In HRLR do not trail the stop to each swing (gets run); if managed, stop to cover costs only; be satisfied with the first objective. Reclaimed FVG after a stop-trail is the typical trap (@114:25, @116:56, @123:37)
- MMXM: Day-type map: (a) news day, non-Monday, clear draw + big ORG (>= 45 handles, 75 preferred) + clustered NWOG/NDOG -> LRLR, aggressive, trade it; (b) no-news Monday -> consolidation or choppy delivery, pre-market only; "Seek and Destroy" = the aggressive extreme of the high-resistance chop profile (not today's) (@120:00, @148:47)
- CTX: Manipulation logic: news drivers are smoke screen; algo puts retail on the wrong side (Judas swing), establishes the session high/low within first ~30 min, then runs fast so trapped traders cannot exit (@129:15, @130:17)
- CTX: Lunch (11:30-13:30) typically reverses/consolidates the AM move; PM resumes AM direction after 13:30 (@07:31)
- EX: Mon 28 Oct 2024 NQ 15s/1m: big gap up at 9:30 over prior settlement, relative equal highs pre-open, one-candle 1m sell-side imbalance above; price filled ORG half-gap within 19 min (by ~9:49), later hit NWOG high ~11:43 after lots of overlapping chop; a 9:37 bearish FVG short was tested by heat before working (@77:32, @93:55, @120:32)
- skip: ~60% of the lecture is psychology (low expectations, $100-250/day or 10-handle targets, journaling, leaderboards/prop firm payout advice, withdrawals, social media). Only the rules above are operational.

## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules:
  1. Large-range-day filter: today's range < average of last 3 (or 5) daily ranges, or an inside day => next day (or 2nd) expansion likely; direction from where smooth equal highs/lows rest (048 @07:33, @97:57).
  2. LRLR vs HRLR classifier by candle overlap: HRLR = each bar shares > ~half of prior bar range, FVGs/OBs get re-touched; LRLR = separated large bars, open imbalances, no re-touch (049 @114:56, @122:36).
  3. Day-type priors: news-driver day (8:30 / first AM hour, not Monday) => LRLR climate; no-news Monday => HRLR in 9:30 AM, trade only 07:00-09:00 pre-market (049 @12:12, @128:44).
  4. Grade the dealing range: swing high (bodies) to discount low, EQ 50%, quadrants; for longs only FVG/OB at or below the lower quadrant (mostly below) and adds only at/below EQ; shorts mirror (048 @67:05, @71:44).
  5. Order-block definition: lowest-body down-close candle(s) before displacement; MT = 50% of body; CE only for gaps/wicks (048 @74:50).
  6. Institutional order-flow entry drill: limit at CE/upper-quadrant of an FVG expected to hold open in LRLR, stop just beyond the gap-forming candle (048 @82:33, @84:35).
  7. ORG half-gap rule: ~70% fills half the 16:00-to-9:30 gap within first 30 min; clustered unfilled NWOG/NDOG beyond it set the day's draw; exit at first (highest/lowest) cluster edge (049 @74:10, @111:55).
  8. FVG stop: candle-2 high/low +1 tick in LRLR; candle-1 extreme +1 tick when close to opposing HTF array or in HRLR; no stop trailing in HRLR, loose/limit-based management in LRLR (049 @100:42, 048 @99:32).
  9. Time: macros are the 10 min before/after each hour (8:30 the only half-hour; 15:15-15:45 last hour), lunch 11:30-13:30 reversal/consolidation, 10:30-11:30 AM higher low; first 30 and 60 min opening ranges, no 15-min one (048 @72:16, 049 @105:15).
  10. Range-break projection: after the prior range high breaks, ~25% of prior range as first draw / turtle-soup frame (051 @01:07).
- Definition changes vs DRAFT:
  - L5/L6 (LRLR/HRLR): not about opposing PD arrays between price and pool, but about delivery texture (overlap of consecutive candles) plus calendar climate; DRAFT proxy "no FVG/OB between" is incomplete.
  - P6 OB: bodies only, lowest-body down-close among the series; MT = 50% of body (not "CE").
  - P5 NWOG: Fri 16:59 close to Sun 18:00 open; CE/quadrants; clusters of 2+ overlapping unfilled gaps within the last ~5 days/weeks are the magnet.
  - S3: dealing range graded with quadrants (25/75), entries <= lower quadrant for longs, EQ cap for pyramiding.
  - P1 stop rule (candle 2 vs candle 1) not in DRAFT.
  - T5 macros: 10 min before and after the top of every hour; 8:30 half-hour; 15:15-15:45 (more general than DRAFT "xx:50-yy:10").
