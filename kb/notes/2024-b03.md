- DEF: NDOG = Thursday-style 17:00 NY settlement close vs 18:00 re-open (one hour apart); NWOG = Friday close vs Sunday 18:00 open. NDOGs have a working life of roughly five days (a convention, not a hard limit); both act as magnets/draws and are the first inputs to bias (@28:19, @29:32, @49:19)
- DEF: Quadrants of a gap (CE and upper/lower quarter) are only subdivided when the gap is >= ~20 handles on NQ; smaller gaps are used whole (@28:55, @29:32)
- DEF: Consequent encroachment (CE) = midpoint of a gap/FVG/volume imbalance; mean threshold (MT) = midpoint of a single-candle range such as an OB. Gap measurement uses candle BODY close to next open, not wicks, so a body-to-body volume imbalance is included in the gap (@72:42, @81:22)
- DEF: Bearish OB = an UP-closing candle; validation = a later candle trades/closes below that candle's OPEN. The open of the OB candle is the "change in state of delivery" level. Wick-below not required if the narrative fits (@66:55, @71:01, @80:11)
- RULE: Bearish OB execution: sell on the candle that opens below the OB open and ticks back up to the OB open; entry zone = OB open down to its low (wick); stop at the OB candle's close ("rejection block") or at worst the MT of the OB; heat up to the initial buy-side equal highs is acceptable (@71:35, @100:30, @102:06)
- RULE: Lowest-threshold entry for shorts into an FVG/inversion FVG: use the LOW (bottom) of the gap as the limit price for a high-probability fill rather than waiting for CE/top; accepts slightly lower precision for fill certainty (@52:06, @102:06)
- RULE: Half-gap read: in a sell program, bodies respect only the lower half of a bearish FVG/IFVG while the upper half stays unfilled (wicks poke at most 1 tick above). Upper half left open = array respected and displacement lower expected; if price closes fully through the gap, expect failure (@74:50, @78:04, @134:00)
- RULE: If price trades up to the top of a premium array and gives no immediate rejection, do not chase; wait for displacement away plus a new FVG on a lower TF (e.g., 1m/30s/15s) and enter there (price delivery continuum; entry TF can be lowered to resync) (@79:09, @136:00)
- RULE: Adding to a position when a fresh FVG forms does NOT justify aggressively trailing the stop; trail only after price has proven itself and rolled away (@102:40, @103:15)
- RULE: Partials and risk: take a first partial at an intermediate level, then move stop to cover cost; after that the trade cannot lose. Once stop moved, never widen it. Take profits at the planned level (here NWOG/NDOG area) rather than chasing the lowest low (@18:18, @45:20, @84:35, @118:30, @214:00)

- DEF: Other macros named: midnight-01:00 and the final hour of the day (details withheld); the 90-minute "cycle" is rejected - 90 min is only the minimum window guaranteeing one macro (@189:13, @182:32)
- RULE: Within a macro the available reaction points are exactly one of {gap/FVG, order block, short-term high/low}; at most two of the three coexist at the same spot, never all three. If there is no gap, use an OB; if neither, use the short-term low/high (@169:11, @170:12, @172:15)

- DEF: Breakaway FVG: an FVG created by displacement out of a consolidation that followed a stop run (price swept the consolidation lows, then left above its high). Such a gap tends to stay at least partly open, acting like real support (bullish) / resistance (bearish); the lower half is least likely to be filled (@128:31, @233:00, @274:10)
- RULE: Bullish IOFED (buy) when you expect an FVG to stay open: limit buy at (or 1 tick above) the candle low that forms the top-side gap boundary (the bottom of the gap = top candle's low), i.e. any trade 1 tick into the gap fills; best fills are between gap CE and top; stop below the high of the first candle (below the gap); accept the draw-down to CE or the gap's lower edge. ## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules:
  1. Macro = xx:50 to xx:10 every hour NY; price spools to a short-term high/low or an inefficiency inside it; at least one of {gap, OB, short-term extreme} exists as the reaction point, never all three.
  2. Session-start frame: from 07:00 (or later start) note first 30-min high/low; wait for post-start relative equal highs/lows to form, be pierced, then trade the reversal into the gap cluster (NWOG/NDOGs) on the opposite side.
  3. Bias seed: price above NWOG and a cluster of recent NDOGs below = sell-side delivery; reverse for bullish.
  4. Bearish OB: up-close candle whose open is later traded/closed through; enter between OB open and its low on an uptick; stop at OB close (rejection block) / MT at worst; consecutive same-colour candles = one OB with the first/highest open as the key price.
  5. IOFED: only when an FVG is expected to remain open (breakaway out of consolidation); limit at/1 tick inside the gap edge nearest the move, stop beyond the first candle, expect heat to CE.
  6. Gap/FVG respect read: bodies hold one half, wicks can pierce to about MT/1 tick; closing fully through = failure.
  7. News mornings (CPI/PPI/NFP): skip pre-08:30 liquidity labeling; trade only after the open, Silver Bullet or macro windows; Monday only after NFP Friday or one early attempt.
  8. Minimum run: need a 20-handle NQ one-sided, predetermined-target run to justify a trade; do not buy the tail of an extended move.
  9. Management: first partial, then stop to cost, never widen; 1% max risk, 1 trade per day, a loss ends the day; no revenge trade.
  10. Daily reference levels for draws: PDH/PDL, last 3 days, previous week H/L, quadrants/midpoint of those ranges, daily volume imbalance CE.
- Definition changes vs DRAFT:
  - P5 NDOG/NWOG: confirmed as 17:00 close vs 18:00 open (NDOG) and Fri close vs Sun 18:00 (NWOG); CE/quadrants only if >= ~20 handles; NDOG life ~5 days.
  - P1/P4: CE defined for gaps (midpoint), MT for single-candle ranges (OB); gap size measured body-to-body including volume imbalance.
  - P6 OB: the OB's key price is the candle OPEN (change in state of delivery); validated by trade/close through that open; multiple consecutive same-colour candles form a single OB; DRAFT "open-to-extreme or body" is superseded.
  - P9 rejection block here = the OB candle's close, used as the stop.
  - S2 CISD = OB open price (not a separate concept; the open of the delivering candle/series).
  - T5 Macros: now precise (xx:50-xx:10 each hour); 90-min cycles dismissed.
  - T1: 07:00 start frame (first 30 min of range) and 08:30-news handling.
