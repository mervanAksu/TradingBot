## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules (≤10):
  1. Volume imbalance (body-to-body space) stays active until one body covers its whole range; judged on the highest TF where it appears (046@23:18).
  2. Trade abort: a close with its body outside the entry FVG/imbalance, followed by a candle trading beyond that candle's extreme, kills the trade before the full stop (045@84:10, @149:00).
  3. Long-support test: the nearest 3 discount arrays (FVG, volume imbalance, OB mean threshold) must hold on body closes; losing all three = reversal/take half (045@190:00, @205:50).
  4. Sick Sister: buy (sell) the lagging index (NQ) at a discount arrays cluster in OTE/below EQ after YM/ES made the extreme and NQ did not; works in consolidation, only continuation info in trends (045@104:50, 053@04:17).
  5. Framework needs ~10 ES handles of room (target 5) for a Silver Bullet; 20 NQ handles equals ES 5 handles (046@33:10, 059@20:42, 049@04:37).
  6. Intermarket: DXY inverse to ES; YM/NQ must agree; if one index already ran its sell side, a second break must show speed/distance or take ~50% partial (046@64:26, @97:49).
  7. Partials first, stop to breakeven only after a partial; scale-in 6+3+1 (046@82:27, 050@49:50).
  8. NWOG/NDOG are persistent; holiday-shortened Friday NWOG is ignored; keep last ~5 NWOG; fill once is not completion because both sides must be delivered (046@16:37, 050@30:16).
  9. Lunch macro 11:50-12:10 runs against the AM move's stops; noon = midday reversal/take-profit; last-hour 15:00 move back into the RTH gap (046@147:16, 050@52:22, 059@43:15).
  10. Limit targets one tick beyond the reference extreme (low 4071.75 -> 4071.50; 4101.25 close needs 4101.50) (050@54:56, @33:22).
- Definition changes vs DRAFT (≤10):
  - P4/volume imbalance: body-to-body space with filled wicks plus a persistence rule (open until a body spans it), CE usable as target.
  - P5: NWOG/NDOG measured from 17:00 close to 18:00 open; NWOG from Friday close to Sunday 18:00 open; persistent, holiday-shortened weeks excluded.
  - P3 BPR: a buy-side imbalance traded through then reversed through the same area becomes BPR (wall).
  - P9 rejection block: lowest close (not extreme wick) among down-close candles with equal lows; used for conservative partial.
  - S2/MSS: shift is "swing low broken", not necessarily CISD; Silver Bullet FVG may be formed pre-10:00 or in-hour.
  - L8/ORG: "opening range gap" = 16:00 close vs 09:30 open; its high is the 09:30 open (data-high usage).
  - L9: Sick Sister variant of SMT (expect the lagging index to catch up) rather than reversal sign.
  - S3: premium/discount for wicks: premium wick above body, discount wick below body; CE of daily wick as draw.
