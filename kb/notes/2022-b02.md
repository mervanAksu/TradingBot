- RULE: Annotate on the HTF (daily) first, then transpose each level to the intraday chart. Every bias must name a specific target (draw): an old low/high pool plus an HTF FVG beyond it. Bias is stated before 9:30 (@05:47, @10:22)
- DEF: Sweeping relative equal lows is NOT automatically a reversal. In a bearish-day narrative the run below EQL is a continuation toward a deeper pool and then an HTF FVG; "equal lows then go up" is rejected as a blanket rule (@06:17)
- RULE: Day-direction anchor = midnight New York open. Bearish day: sell at or above the midnight open (premium of the day); the Judas swing is the rally above the open that takes short-term buy stops, then price breaks below short-term lows and trades down into the target (@10:22, @43:52)
- RULE: Short setup at 8:30 (bearish day, price above midnight open): wait for price to take a short-term high (buy side), trade back down through a short-term low (displacement), leave an FVG, sell the retrace into that FVG, aim at the nearest sell-side pool. Take profit at +5 ES points as the floor, optionally hold the rest to the pool (@46:26, @47:27, @57:06)
- RULE: Trade windows: 8:30 (news embargo lifts), 9:30 (equity open), PM session from 13:30, and the 15:00-16:00 market-on-close macro; one live attempt in the morning (8:30, else 9:30 if 8:30 failed or lost), one in the PM; after a morning win the PM is paper only (@39:16, @66:48, @72:54)
- RULE: Level alone is useless without time; time alone is useless unless price sits at a PD array. Entry requires both (@33:34, @36:40)
- RULE: Lunch-hour raid (12:00-13:00 NY): in a bearish day, expect the lunch-hour highs / short-term buy-side pool to be run at about 13:00 to flush early shorts and fill sell orders; aggressive entry on the sweep, or wait for PM (@16:36, @17:08, @18:11)
- DEF: Bearish breaker (this lecture): buy-side pool is run, then price reverses and trades back below the prior short-term low; the last down-close candle before the run up is the zone; short when price returns to it. Valid only if a draw (pool below) is known beforehand, else it is a guess (@20:13, @20:44)
- MMXM: Partial-and-stop management: take first partial when price breaks below the first short-term low; second partial plus stop to slightly better than break-even; exit the next block (three contracts) when price trades above the up-close candle that should have capped the move (@61:12, @62:15, @63:16)
- RULE: Failure signal for a short: an up-close candle (bearish order block) that should cap price is traded through, especially after multiple sell-side levels were already taken without follow-through; then buy side above is the new draw, and price can retake the midnight open (@64:46, @66:18)
- CTX: PM-session precondition: first decide what the daily range is doing (expand up, expand down, reversed in the AM, or waiting for next-day news). Large-range day can appear in the PM from a quiet AM (@15:36, @16:06)
- CTX: On a heavy-news day, daily-range target stayed one-sided; price consolidated pre-open, swept below equal lows toward 4070, then ran to the daily FVG (4040-4100 area) and closed inside it (@01:44, @15:06, @22:21)
- CTX: Day-of-news handling: trade medium/high-impact news days or none, but reduce risk size when practising on news (@42:52, @43:22)
- DEF: Time hierarchy per this lecture: time of day, day of week, week of month, month, season; price delivery is algorithmic, so "buy/sell pressure" is not the driver (@32:02, @33:03)

### 041 | 20220624 | Final lecture: AM review (Powell day), OTE long example, risk sizing, drawdown scaling, stop-trail rule (ES Sep 2022)

- RULE: Long entry template (flagship): price trades down into an FVG, shows a short-term MSS up, leaves an FVG with an order block (last down-close candle) to its left; buy with a limit in the OTE zone (62-70% retrace of the leg, below equilibrium), here 1 tick (0.25) under that candle's low (3754.75). Stop = low of the leg (3745.75), 9 pts risk (@18:10, @18:40, @19:10)
- RULE: Targets in order: first imbalance / candle high (3780.25, +25.5 pts = about 2.8R), then the short-term high and buy-side pool, final daily-range target (3805) (@19:41, @33:06, @29:24)
- RULE: Rejection of a candidate FVG: if price trades into an FVG but the prior short-term low was not broken with displacement, skip the setup (@11:57, @12:28)
- RULE: Position size = (equity x risk%) / (stop points x $/pt). Example: 10k, 1%, 9 pts, ES $50 pt does not fit, so use MES ($5): 11 contracts' worth rounds down to 2 MES; risk 90 USD; commissions ignored in the sizing (@22:14, @23:15, @23:45, @24:46)
- RULE: Drawdown scaling: after a loss, risk half (1% -> 0.5% -> 0.25%) and return to full risk only after regaining about 50% of the loss. After a 2% loss, next trade 1% (@31:25, @31:56, @41:34)
- RULE: Stop trail (static): when price has covered 50% of entry-to-target distance, move stop to 25% of the entry-to-stop distance (trim risk only partly); at 75% of the distance move stop to break-even; no earlier break-even. Example: entry 3754.75, target 3780.25, so 12.5 pts = 50% (@46:08, @46:38, @47:09)
- RULE: Partials (multi-position): fixed profit step of 15-20 points per contract, take something off every time; left to the trader to tune (@47:39, @48:09)
- RULE: Always place a hard stop at entry; a "mental" stop is rejected (@15:00, @40:34, @41:04)
- RULE: Two-order version: both at the same OTE entry; first targets the near imbalance (+25.5 pts), second targets the short-term high / daily range target (@28:52, @29:24)
