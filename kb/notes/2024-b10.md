### 029 | 20240917 | How To Manage Missed Entries (day before FOMC, NQ)
tags: opening range gap (ORG), first presented FVG, consequent encroachment, mean threshold, breaker, relative equal highs (L4), SMT (L9), NWOG, partials, 20-handle filter
- RULE: "ORG midpoint" bias model. Gap = 09:30 first print vs prior RTH settle (last print 16:14/16:15 NY). Valid only if gap >= ~40 handles (bigger = better; ~100 is the sweet spot; > ~200 skip - likely continuation/manipulation). Gap up -> bias short, gap down -> bias long; target = gap midpoint (50%, fib anchored open to prior settle), stated ~70% hit rate within the opening-range window 09:30-10:00 (@05:53, @33:31, @69:02, @70:02)
- RULE: Entry = first FVG formed on the 1-min chart after the open (candle 1 of the FVG cannot be the 09:30 bar; earliest possible is the 09:31 bar's gap). Enter via limit anywhere inside the FVG: aggressive = 1 tick beyond candle-3 extreme; passive = CE or deeper side of the gap. Stop = beyond candle 2 (short: above its high; long: below its low) (@55:39, @56:11, @65:2x, @134:35)
- RULE: Management: once price breaks the nearest short-term low/high, move stop to cost, take a partial at that short-term extreme; remainder to the midgap (can take 75-80% off at midgap and let the rest try for full gap close with a free stop) (@57:43, @58:43)
- RULE: Execution drill: on 15s/1m, mark relative equal highs/lows (short-term buy/sell-side), take the FVG after the run into them, stop at candle 2, partials at the near extremes, stop to cost, then stop trails to the prior low; target ~20-25 handles minimum - if the entry-to-target range cannot frame >= 20 handles, do not trade (@93:10, @128:32)
- RULE: Sub-minute "high frequency" version needs no daily bias but suffers most stop-outs; filter it with time-of-day, day-of-week, week-of-month, seasonality and HTF order flow / HTF draw (@47:56, @50:00)
- DEF: Midpoint naming: order block / breaker midpoint = mean threshold; midpoint of a gap or wick = consequent encroachment (@109:18)
- DEF: SMT compare on candle bodies, not wicks: a swing high whose wick is higher but whose bodies are lower in the second index counts as divergence; compare ES vs NQ (same, current contract month), Dow only as early warning; use the TF above the execution TF (@39:09, @41:13, @76:13)
- DEF: Wick clusters (several overlapping wicks) treated as a PD array / liquidity pool ("grain pools"); price returns to them in a way tied to bodies (@44:21)
- DEF: Equal highs on the HTF carry across contract rolls: translate the old-contract level to the new contract (offset = contract premium) (@42:45)
- CTX: Day before FOMC: choppy, imprecise, PD arrays get run through ("coloured outside the lines"); be selective, let price spike through the FVG first and enter inside the resulting wick; avoid FOMC day itself (2 pm run one way, ~14:30 presser reverses) (@12:27, @31:58, @32:29)
- CTX: A "buy program" tell: price grinds up with bodies staying inside the breaker, no FVGs or inefficiencies, immediate rebalances only; shorting such a market is painful, so no short when no displacement / FVG shows (@106:08, @111:21)
- MMXM: Breaker rules used live: low-high-lower-low sequence marks a breaker, the last up-close candle before the lower low; below its mean threshold stay short-only, above it do not short; first FVG then entry back into breaker for ~20 handles. Short signal inside lower half of a deep range (selling discount) rejected (@162:47, @172:12, @183:44)
- EX: 2024-09-17 NQ gap-up ~100+ handles (settle 19,661.75 style level, mid ~19,728): short at first FVG formed 09:33 (candle 2 stop), reached lower quadrant, midgap delivered before 10:00 (@53:08, @97:22)
- EX: Afternoon drill on 15s: entry in FVG ~19,745, two partials, stop rolled down, rejection block low as target (@218:44, @228:32)

### 031 | 20240919 | Micro vs. Mini (huge gap-up, NQ Dec)
tags: opening range gap (ORG), first presented FVG, inversion FVG, balanced price range, 2022 model, turtle soup, fib extensions, macros (T5), 20-handle filter, order block (P6)
- RULE: Gap size filter again: ~40-60 handles = tradeable ORG entry; a several-hundred-handle gap = do not enter at the open; allow price to make/raise the initial high of the day first (retail breakout-buy trap), then work the first 1-min FVG formed after that run (@07:08, @09:29, @09:59)
- RULE: "2022 model" pattern: two stacked FVGs where a small one sits just above a larger one - after the first FVG is tapped expect price to stab into the higher one (@21:20, @35:36)
- RULE: Minimum-range filter for any PD-array entry (FVG/OB/etc.): entry-to-target must be >= 20 handles (NQ); if 18, skip. Example box: from a lower-quadrant entry to a minor sell-side low (@28:56, @29:29, @38:09, @39:10)
- RULE: Short-after-inversion sequence: price leaves an inversion FVG, taps its CE/ body once, then the NEXT displacement lower that prints a fresh FVG is the entry; if the retest candle closes back inside / no new FVG forms, no trade (@38:39, @41:13, @43:17)
- RULE: Beginner target: 1 micro, take profit at $75-100 (framed in dollars, i.e. ~40-50 NQ points at $2/pt) with stop moved to cost once in profit; stop trading after one such win/day (@22:21, @48:56, @49:28)
- RULE: After a big up-gap, the "range-stop-run" logic: first high = initial high of day (IHOD); the run above it that traps breakout buyers forms a balanced price range (BPR) of the run + its reversal; expect sell-side below the low of the trap and measure with fib of that swing high->low: -0.25 first stop, -0.5 half deviation, -1 = 1 SD measured move; OR upper-quadrant level of the ORG as intermediate target (@53:04, @54:08, @54:38, @56:13)
- RULE: Fib-confluence recipe: a 62-79% retracement alone is nothing; require a PD array (OB / FVG) inside that zone AND a macro time window (last 10 min of the hour into the first 10 min of the next) (@79:51, @80:21)
- DEF: Macro = 20-min window: final 10 min of each hour plus first 10 min of next ("time and price theory"), every hour; 09:50-10:10 used here. Order block = two price levels (entry level and an add level, presumably open and midpoint/mean threshold), not a zone of orders (@73:41, @76:18)
- DEF: Contract roll: switch from Sept to Dec NQ on the Tuesday of roll week; carry old-contract levels (Aug 27-29 hourly equal highs) across with the contract spread (@04:55)
- DEF: Fib used only for range measurement: retracements inside range and extensions (-0.25, -0.5, -1) outside; value only when another PD array agrees (@79:21)
- CTX: Retail trap: breakout above initial high after a huge gap = stops/longs trapped; ICT expects an initial dip (oversold look), run above IHOD, then the real drop back to ORG upper quadrant / midgap. Support/resistance equivalents are the BPR formed by that swept high (old high becomes support after the run) (@09:59, @20:20, @62:21)
- CTX: Time-of-day: first setup is always in 09:31-10:00; data arrays = prior settle (16:14 print) and 09:30 open; if/else bias: open above settle -> bias down to midpoint; no setup -> do nothing (@67:58, @69:00)
- EX: 2024-09-19 NQ Dec 1m/15s: gap up several hundred handles from 19,6xx; ORG midpoint ~19,843 (micro 19,844.5); IHOD swept, first FVG printed, entry in FVG near highs yielded $75-100/micro; ~20-handle run box (@01:02, @12:08, @26:24)

### 032 | 20240920 | CFDs vs. Futures (Friday, small ORG, NQ Dec)

- RULE: Data points to log every day: prior-day RTH settle (16:14 bar close) and today's 09:30 open; ORG = the span between them. Its high / upper quadrant / CE / low are used as reference prices ("not a zone"). If today opens inside yesterday's FPFVG, that older gap outranks today's small one (@17:51, @18:21, @21:59, @29:44)
- RULE: Short template from the example: today open lies inside yesterday's FPFVG; bias lower; at 09:50 macro start, enter on a rally back to the high/CE of yesterday's FPFVG (the algorithm "sells on an uptick"); target = the relative equal lows / sell-side below (exit a few ticks beyond). Stop trailed behind candles; later target = CE of a discount wick (@47:31, @50:04, @52:08, @55:43, @61:21)
- RULE: Macro behaviour: from xx:50 to yy:10 (20 min) the price engine "spools" - consolidation before it (time distortion) is not indecision; expect delivery toward the nearest un-run liquidity pool in the direction implied by context (@13:09, @33:58, @38:13)
- RULE: In the 09:31-10:00 window, the first FVG may be drawn including or excluding its volume imbalance: for CFD feeds always include the body-to-body volume imbalance in the gap (price feeds differ); for futures the pure wick gap (candle1 high to candle3 low) is the base, imbalance optional (@12:38, @46:30, @66:09)

- DEF: Time distortion = the choppy/consolidative stretch before a macro; the 09:30-10:00 interval = "opening range"; ORG = settle-to-open gap, called discount ORG when open < settle (@08:03, @13:41)
- DEF: Breakaway gap = a volume imbalance (body gap) left by the move that leaves the area, used to confirm the algorithmic leg is under way; fair value gap must be a true 3-candle gap, a lone rectangle between candles is not (@47:31, @27:40)
- DEF: Order flow tools (DOM/level 2/bookmap) dismissed as non-causal; liquidity = prior swing highs/lows, not visible resting orders (@35:00)
- DEF: NQ tick math: 1 handle = 4 ticks = $20 per mini, $2 per micro (@04:52)
- CTX: TGIF: Friday often retraces ~20-30% of the weekly range; Fri + small ORG + big range prior day = harder morning, favour waiting for the 09:50 macro (@16:19, @31:19)
- CTX: Pre-open sequence seen: run of pre-session buy side at open (traps shorts, then traps breakout buyers), return to opening price (= low of the ORG), second pass at buy side, then drop (@15:17, @26:35)
- CTX: Never fade a news day without experience; Monday also avoided for beginners (@28:42)

### 033 | 20240923 | How To Avoid Seek & Destroy - Tape Reading (Monday, NQ)
tags: time distortion, no-trade filter, first presented FVG, silver bullet (T1), inversion FVG, consequent encroachment, volume imbalance, ORG, relative equal highs (L4), LRLR (L5), turtle soup (L7)
- RULE: No-trade filter "held market": if price keeps spending time INSIDE gaps / inefficiencies / wick bodies instead of above old highs or below old lows, the market is being held (time distortion); skip until it releases (typically after 10:00 silver bullet hour or afternoon). Count: repeated re-entry into the FPFVG after its CE was already tagged is a red flag (@41:12, @41:42, @48:31)
- RULE: A PD array alone is not a signal: require displacement away from the level after the tap (the "signature"), then trade the next FVG/OB retest. Entry sequence for longs in Caleb's model: short-term high taken (buy side), short-term low taken (sell side), then rally using a FVG (or IFVG) with entry near wick CE; target short-term buy side, exit around the -0.25 extension of the low-to-high move allowing for spread (@07:46, @16:00, @17:01)
- RULE: After an FVG's CE has been touched, price should leave and not revisit; the upper half of a bullish gap (when long) should not be traded back to, and CE should not be re-hit, until the sell-side low is taken - a repeat pattern worth journaling as its own model (@51:16, @60:07)
- RULE: Preferred pre-liquidity setup: enter on the return to premium BEFORE the sell-side low is taken (low-resistance run) rather than after it was already swept (@06:13)
- RULE: Close/violation test: an inversion FVG is considered failed (remove from chart) once a candle body closes through the far side; wicks through are tolerated (@43:43, @39:10)
- RULE: Wait for 10:00 silver bullet and use the first FVG there when morning conditions are muddy (@14:58, @22:15)
- DEF: Volume imbalance (body-to-body gap) sitting on top of an FVG adds confluence when price shows respect at it (@38:09)
- DEF: Weekly/daily levels used: previous-week gap (premium/ discount arrays), old daily buy side (Aug 22 2024 high ~20,251.25), old new-day-opening-gap overlapping FPFVG; medium news at 09:45 (Flash PMI) = noise (@20:54, @10:26, @29:06)
- CTX: Day-type: Monday + large gap + unfilled ORG portion + chop + news flurry = hard morning; expected cleaner afternoon after the hold releases. Conditions "must be one-sided" for a high-probability trade; if you can imagine it going either way, sit out (@35:20, @36:57, @61:38)
- CTX: Only levels already on prior notes appear on chart; do not add during session (@43:13)
- EX: 2024-09-23 NQ 1m/15s: ORG midpoint tagged early, FPFVG only 0.25 pt, price consolidated 09:30-10:30; ICT declined every long/short; a 15-handle long was skipped and would have stopped (@60:07)

## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules:
  1. ORG model: gap = 09:30 open vs 16:14 settle; >= ~40 handles (best 40-100, skip > ~200); target = gap 50% within 09:30-10:00, ~70% claimed; entry = first 1-min FVG formed 09:31+ (never the 09:30 bar), stop beyond candle 2 (029/031).
  2. Entry inside the FVG by limit: aggressive 1 tick beyond candle-3 extreme; or CE / deeper; after short-term low breaks: stop to cost + partial (029@57:43).
  3. Minimum 20-handle (NQ) entry-to-target range else no trade (029@93:10, 031@38:09).
  4. Yesterday's FPFVG extended right (~3-day life, sometimes weeks); use its high/UQ/CE/LQ/low as reference prices (032@17:51-21:59).
  5. Macro window xx:50-yy:10 (20 min): consolidation before = "time distortion"; delivery to nearest pool inside; 09:50-10:10 first one (032@13:41, 031@73:41).
  6. Held-market no-trade filter: price lingering inside gaps/inefficiencies, re-tagging FPFVG CE = skip (033@41:12).
  7. Inversion FVG long/short pattern: tap leaves lowest part open, bodies in upper half (035@47:01).
  8. SMT by bodies not wicks; ES vs NQ, Dow only warning (029@39:09).
  9. Trap model: after big gap-up expect run above IHOD (BPR/ trapped breakout longs) then drop; measure with fib -0.25/-0.5/-1 of swing (031@54:08).
  10. TGIF Friday retraces ~20-30% of weekly range (032@16:49).
- Definition changes vs DRAFT:
  - FVG for CFD feeds includes volume imbalance; futures base = wick gap (032@66:09).
  - Mean threshold = OB midpoint; CE = midpoint of gap or wick (029@109:18); P9 wick CE used as target/entry.
  - Volume imbalance (P4) is weakest array; OB > FVG > VI (035@22:01).
  - L9 SMT: compare bodies (029@41:13).
  - T5 Macros: 20 min, xx:50 to yy:10, "every hour", described as scripted algorithm legs (031@73:41).
  - L8 settle time: RTH settle = 16:14 NY bar close (16:15 print), 09:30 open (029@24:00, 032@07:33).
  - Order block described as two prices (entry + add) not a zone (031@76:18).
