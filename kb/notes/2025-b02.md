## Batch summary
Top mechanical rules
- Framing range: 20 daily candles high-low (40/60 only in parabolic markets), graded 0/25/50/75/100; work zone 25-75; extremes ignored until a daily close breaches (004).
- Grade every daily wick (premium: body-top to high; discount: low to body-bottom), every FVG/suspension block/gap and every opening-range-gap into 25/50/75; project forward to LTF (004,006,007,009).
- High-probability FVG/OB = one edge or CE of it lies on a graded quadrant of a wick/gap/ORG/range; quadrant crossing the gap middle = common gap, skip. Time must agree: macro window (e.g. 07:50-08:10, 10:50-11:10, 14:50-15:10), first 30 min of RTH, or session open (004,006,007).
- Bullish strength = FVG/BISI left unfilled (upper quadrant/CE untouched); never buy a full closure; exit if price trades below the upper quadrant (stop at CE or 1 tick below); re-enter after a LTF shift with an unfilled LTF gap (006).
- Order-block series: zone = consecutive opposite-close candles; bullish OB must hold from MT up, bearish from low to MT; bodies matter more than wicks (004).
- Wick/PDA flip: premium wick below price? discount wick above price becomes premium array; premium wick becomes discount array when price is above; pick the lowest-reaching wick among a stack (006,007,009).
- Liquidity pool qualifier: right-hand equal high lower than left (buyside) / right low higher (sellside); body close beyond = run completed, wick-only = unfinished business (006,007).
- RTH ORG = prior 16:14 ET close to 09:30 open; carry today plus previous 3 days' ORG quadrants; ORG of a day whose body closes above the prior swing/ATH is highest priority (007,008).
- Staged distribution at an ATH: day body above old high, then each higher close = further staging level; failure = bodies cannot hold above CE of key candle/ORG, then macro-time drop through inversion FVG (007).
- Breakaway/measuring gap classification = unfilled gap sitting between two quadrants of the graded PDA (007).

Definition changes vs DRAFT
- S7/S3: dealing range is a fixed 20-candle daily window with 25/50/75 grading, not a free swing choice (004).
- P9 rejection block / wick PDAs: wicks are graded like gaps (low/25/50/75/high), reused after being filled, with premium/discount flip rules (006,007).
- P4/P5: ORG anchored 16:14 ET RTH close to 9:30 open (not 17:00-18:00 NDOG); fully used with quadrants (008).
- P6 OB: the series of opposite candles with MT, "order" = ordering the change in state of delivery; strict version = open of lowest down-close candle (004).
- P2 IFVG / inversion: every PDA has an inversion aspect; inverted use targets CE (007).
- P1 FVG: only gaps lying on quadrants are high probability; first-presented FVG must be displacive, not tick-sized (004,009).
- Suspension block: one candle with imbalances on both sides, annotated imbalance-low to imbalance-high (007,009).
- L7/L4: sweep needs a body beyond the pool; wick-only is unfinished business (006).
- T5: macro windows quoted in this batch: 06:50-07:10 (observed), 07:50-08:10, 10:50-11:10, 14:50-15:10 (xx:50-xx:10 holds).
- First 30 minutes (not 15) = opening range.
