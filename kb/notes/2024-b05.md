## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules:
  1. OR = 09:30-10:00 NY; ORG = prior RTH settle (16:15) to 09:30 first print; NWOG = Fri settle vs Sun 18:00 open; carry unused OR/ORG levels 3 days, NWOG for weeks; use CE and 25/75% quadrants of each.
  2. TGIF: fib the week range; 20-30% retrace expected from Thu 13:00 NY through Fri; once 20% is printed and flat, stand aside for the rest of the week.
  3. Lunch macro 11:30-13:30: after a one-way run, expect retrace to the easiest EQL/swing low formed after 10:00 (mirror for downs).
  4. IOFED: enter at the near edge of the FVG (+1 tick beyond the formation candle's extreme as buffer), expect partial gap fill/hold; gap held open = go; stop beyond the swept low; exits at lowest threshold (quadrant/EQH), partial at 50% of run when a minor high clears.
  5. Min target size: NQ >= 10 handles (prefer 15, require ~20 to the draw); ES >= 5.
  6. FOMC: 14:00 move reverses ~70% at 14:30, big move runs to ~15:15.
  7. Take the first side of liquidity as the Judas move after 09:30; the opposite side is the draw. Only EQH/EQL formed after 07:00 for the student.
  8. Valid EQH = right-most high slightly lower; wick above with bodies below keeps it valid.
  9. Wick/gap CE test: failing to touch CE of a reference wick/gap = strength; closing through it = failure.
  10. Beginner discipline: no entries 09:30-10:00; 1 contract; one loss ends the day; full-win day ends session.
