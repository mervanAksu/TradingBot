### 001 | 20260901 | How New Students Should Start Part 2 (live hypothetical tape-read, NQ/MNQ)
tags: opening range gap (ORG), first-presented FVG (FPFVG), inversion FVG (P2), volume imbalance (P4), consequent encroachment, octants/quadrants, consolidation profile, partials
- DEF: Opening Range Gap (ORG) = space between the prior RTH settlement (16:15 NY close of cash-index futures session) and the 09:30 RTH open price; distinct from NDOG (18:00 gap). Graded with a fib/octant grid anchored settle->open; CE (50%) is the normal first draw (@64:12, @56:33)
- RULE: ORG CE is expected to trade by ~10:00 NY with ~70% likelihood; if not reached by then it is a deferred delivery and may be skipped on that day (@56:33)
- DEF: Octant/quadrant grading: any range (ORG, daily efficiency box) is cut into 8 (octants) / 4 (quadrants) equal slices; the lowest octant/quadrant edge of the ORG is a target when two independent ranges (ORG grid + daily buy-side-balance/sell-side-efficiency box) share the same price - confluence = preferred objective (@56:33, @57:03, @82:36)
- DEF: First-Presented FVG (FPFVG) = first FVG printed after the 09:30 open (on 1m-5m); a prior day's FPFVG is carried as a key level. Condition for holding: no candle BODIES in its lower half (bull case), wicks allowed; bodies below = failure (@07:24, @17:31, @42:29)
- RULE: Inversion FVG test: a down-close FVG that price trades above and then returns into is treated as support ("foothold") unless a candle CLOSES through it; wick-only trade through = not yet inverted; a close below would flip it into resistance (@15:30, @43:30, @44:46)
- RULE: Confirmation heuristic for a rejection wick: the next candle (or the one after) must close above the wick's 50% (CE) / completely overtake the wick; failure to do so = stop-out likely (@16:57, @19:45, @31:25)
- RULE: Volume imbalance (1-tick body gap between a close and the next open) acts as a small support: want it left unfilled while order flow bullish (@51:26)
- RULE: Trade management used in drill: stop just beyond the low of the entry candle/swing; move to break-even + costs (~5-6 pts on NQ) after first partial; partial at first buy-side pool (equal highs, prior session high), then at the shared-confluence level (29,212 example), trail remainder under last low; pyramid adds at FPFVG/OB retests (@05:53, @21:53, @46:22, @65:13)
- RULE: Re-delivery measuring: a retracement leg is projected above the new high; if price re-trades that range height, trail stop under the retracement low; use as scale-out level (@69:30)
- CTX: Day after a trending (selloff) session -> expect consolidation in AM; big overnight/RTH gap (~400 handles) = bias toward the gap fill (longs preferred in discount near seasonal low) but expect chop; being stopped on the first attempt is normal (@29:42, @41:14, @26:32)
- CTX: 10:30 NY = 1-hour dealing range complete; algos "speed up" after it; expect more range expansion after 10:30 (@42:59, @61:24)
- CTX: Overnight (ETH) prints count less than the same prices traded inside RTH; levels need RTH delivery for significance (@78:16)
