### 028 | 20240916 | How To Trade ICT FVGs Correctly (entry/stop/grading of FVGs, Judas swing, live NQ drill)
tags: FVG (P1), volume imbalance (P4), CE, quadrants, inversion FVG (P2), BPR (P3), OB (P6), mean threshold, wick as PD array, Judas swing, ORG, first-presented FVG, macros, minor/primary BSL-SSL, immediate rebalance
- DEF: FVG = 3 candles; candle 2 is the "trigger/framing" candle whose range is not shared by candle 1's extreme and candle 3's extreme. Bearish: gap = candle-1 low down to candle-3 high; bullish: candle-1 high up to candle-3 low. Always extend the box to include the body-to-body volume imbalance between candles (not just the wick extremes) (@17:20, @24:15, @42:46)
- RULE: Short entry (bearish FVG): limit at candle-3 high minus 1 tick; price enters the gap from below: add at the lower quadrant (first 25% into the gap), then CE, then upper quadrant only if price keeps spending time there (best premium); stop = 1 tick above candle-2 high for max leverage, or above candle 1 for the conservative one-contract trader; wicks through candle-1 low are tolerated, bodies closing beyond are not (@19:57, @20:59, @29:21, @31:22)
- RULE: Long entry (bullish FVG): limit at candle-3 low plus 1 tick; add at upper quadrant, CE (midpoint) and lower quadrant; stop = 1 tick below candle-2 low. If entry missed and price is already below candle-3 low, market in immediately (@40:42, @43:49, @48:30, @49:02)
- RULE: Grading: split every FVG (and every wick, and order block) into quadrants (25/50/75). Valid = price only dips into the half nearest the entry (for bullish: upper half) and leaves the far half untouched; touching CE = weak, closing beyond the far boundary = failed, mean threshold must hold on a closing basis; the faster candle 4 leaves the gap, the stronger (@22:04, @43:17, @53:42, @55:15, @92:12)
- RULE: If a FVG's far half gets traded, expect it to become an inversion FVG (flip colour, orange) and trade it from the other side; a stop-out on a FVG is information, then reverse with half size (@32:56, @52:38, @67:12)
- RULE: Wicks are PD arrays: a wick above price is a premium array, below price a discount array; same grading (CE, quadrants) and the upper half of a bullish wick should keep price from entering the lower half; a gap that sits inside the lower half of a drop is not a short trigger (@56:17, @64:08, @66:41)
- RULE: FVG candle-3 cannot be the 09:30 opening candle; the first presented FVG is 09:31 or later; first-presented FVG and half-ORG are the easy first targets (@74:24)
- RULE: Judas swing filter: if price opens at 09:30 and runs straight in one direction (single swing, no PD arrays inside the leg, e.g. straight drop from the open on a gap-down day) it is the fake move; do not chase it; look for the swing low to be taken then a FVG (bearish-looking, within the lower half of the drop) to FAIL and price to rally to the ORG lower quadrant / CE and buy-side (@61:05, @62:06, @63:38, @69:45)
- RULE: Targets/handles: ORG CE (half-gap) is the first draw, ~70% hit by 10:00; need at least ~20 handles (NQ) between entry and first target (15 handle minimum for the student), 10 handle trades rejected; drill structure = first partial at nearest liquidity, terminus at next (@46:27, @98:04, @107:39, @129:16)
- RULE: Fill: use limit orders for entries and exits; market order only to get out or for the training drill; practise one micro contract (NQ micro $2/pt, ES micro $5/pt, mini $20/pt) for >= 1 month demo, log time-to-target and drawdown (@35:28, @37:05, @102:29)
- RULE: Rollover: switch to December NQ (NQZ4) when the volume/OI data say so, check barchart (@13:34)
- RULE: News: ignore 08:30 medium-impact data; trust price (@08:19)
- MMXM: Primary vs minor liquidity: after the open, a high/low that takes out the initial opening-range extreme becomes primary buy/sell side; earlier swing-point liquidity is minor; taking primary sell-side = much more meaningful (@80:32, @81:03)

- Top mechanical rules:

  2. FVG validity grading: price may enter only the near half; CE touched = weak; far half untouched = strong; failed grade => inversion FVG (028 @43:17, @55:15)

  5. Judas swing: straight leg from the 09:30 open with no PD array in it is fake; fade it to ORG lower quadrant/CE (028 @62:06, @69:45)
  6. Minimum 20 handles (NQ) between entry and first target; skip 10-handle setups (028 @98:04)
  7. Wick CE = PD array (premium above, discount below); bodies stacking at CE after a buy-stop run => entry; stop above bearish OB open (026 @44:00, @67:05)
