## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules
  1. Gap/wick respect test (bearish): after price retraces into a down-close gap/discount wick, it must reach (touch) the 50% CE with only wicks; any body close in the upper half voids it; failure to reach CE = continuation signal; mirror for bulls (116, 118).
  2. IFVG validity: body close through the gap; then retrace allowed to CE; body closes below CE confirm; first FVG after leaving below = entry (119). Wicks alone do not invert (116).
  3. Reclaimed FVG: IFVG that later trades through to the opposite side and holds its far half becomes a reclaimed (original polarity) FVG (117, 118).
  4. New 2h-lunch PD array: carry forward the gap formed immediately before the pre-13:30 lunch liquidity raid; next-day trade into it = reversal (123).
  5. Event horizon = 50% between two same-side liquidity pools (or swept low and next lower low): partial/add level; take ~25% there, keep a runner (119, 122, 123).
  6. Immediate rebalance: open, trade to prior-day wick CE (or high) and leave; delivery that day or next; day-2 trade through it = complex reversal (121).
  7. Gradient levels: octants/quadrants of the RTH ORG and wicks are anchors; OB/FVG entries should sit on them; propulsion block rides a parent OB (122).
  8. Time distortion: unreadable chop -> go up TF until the target imbalance shows (118); time inside a gap: valid arrays are used by 1-2 candles (116).
  9. Rollover week: reduce size, ignore intraday runs, use continuous contract for HTF levels (118, 120).
  10. Macro 9:00-9:10 buy-side run then 9:30 return to an IFVG = short template (122).
