### 018 | 20240827 | Live: opening range gap, first FVG after 9:30, PD-array half rule
tags: opening range gap (ORG), first FVG after 9:30, inversion/reclaimed FVG, NDOG/NWOG cluster, breaker (P7), propulsion block, rejection block (P9), mohawk tolerance, PDL (L1), lunch macro, accumulation
- DEF: Opening Range Gap (ORG) = prior-day settlement (last print 16:14 ET, ES/NQ cash-close reference) to the 9:30 opening print. "Significant" only if larger than ~20-30 handles; its CE (midpoint) is the first threshold to watch (@08:23, @10:31, @21:40)
- RULE: Opening range = the 30 min 09:30-10:00 (not 5/15 min). When ORG is significant, ~70% of the time price trades at least to the ORG CE inside that window; filling toward 3/4 or full gap closure is the usual objective. No directional bias needed to use this (@08:23, @30:43, @51:43)
- RULE: First FVG after the open: the first FVG formed from the 9:31 candle onward to 10:00 (a FVG cannot use the 9:30 candle as its middle bar per ICT's stated rule). Extend it across the whole day; it is the reference for the first inversion/support-resistance test (@37:52, @72:46)
- RULE: FVG role-flip sequence (mechanical): price sells off through the FVG body-close -> it becomes an inversion FVG (resistance); when price later closes back above it with an abrupt structure shift, it is "reclaimed" and is support again; the narrative (bullish/bearish) decides which role to expect (@16:59, @25:29, @64:36, @72:15)
- RULE: PD-array half rule: if bullish, price should respect the upper half of the array (OB/FVG/breaker/imbalance) and not trade into the lower half; trade into the deepest part must reject immediately or the array is a dud - cancel/scratch the trade. Bearish = mirror (should not trade into the upper/premium half) (@83:07, @95:26, @173:51, @197:59)
- RULE: Tolerance: candle bodies may close outside a PD array by about 1-2 ticks on NQ (1 tick on ES); wicks may overshoot a few ticks ("mohawk" = a small wick/body poke outside the array that does not change the read). Use the whole volume-imbalance range (open-to-close gap between candles), not the wick extreme (@81:35, @82:05, @154:20, @154:51)
- RULE: PD-array look-back: use arrays formed within the last 60 trading days; the newest ~20 days are the most price-sensitive. For gap work, only NDOGs within the last 5 days and NWOGs within the last 5 weeks while learning (@86:42, @87:44, @147:43)
- RULE: Clustered/nested NDOG+NWOG levels act as a gravitational pool; do not discard a gap once it has been traded to - the algorithm keeps referring to old ones within the look-back (@61:29, @85:40)
- RULE: Daily-liquidity reference list: highest high / lowest low of the previous 3 days; ignore Sunday's high/low, use Sunday only for the NWOG open. If the session opens in the lower half of the previous day's range, expect a return to PDL; speed/range expansion increases as price approaches the resting stops (@189:18, @200:03, @204:43)
- RULE: Partial-profit logic used in the example: first partial one tick under the easiest nearby low / at the ~50% (CE) of the range, runner to the one-quadrant extension of the range; add on a retest of a PD array inside the move (@20:07, @105:15, @175:22)
- DEF: Propulsion block = a candle that trades into a prior order block (without its body crossing the OB mean threshold) and then is the launching point away from it; breaker = last up/down candle whose body breaks structure after a sweep, here with a long lower wick (use upper half when bullish) (@79:32, @84:07, @160:00, @194:22)
- DEF: Rejection block (wick zone of a swing high/low, CE of the wick used) must be touched once and then not be revisited; same-price highs (e.g. an earlier high equal to a day-low area) show repeated respect (@163:37, @170:18)
- CTX: Smart-money accumulation = time spent below old lows/relative equal lows (sell stops) while an unfilled inefficiency (ORG, NDOG/NWOG) sits above; they never buy breakouts, only discount on sell-side delivery; the drop that sweeps the REL then reverses is the "accumulation" and the reversal runs to the gap/buy-side (@47:35, @57:54, @63:03, @63:35)
- CTX: Session-phase flow: 9:30-10:00 first FVG/ORG test -> expansion with small consolidations (re-accumulation) -> ~11:30-13:30 "lunch" deeper retracement window (London close ~11:30) (@89:48, @90:18)
- MMXM: Only generic accumulate/manipulate/distribute logic here - no explicit Buy/Sell Model material (no SMR, no 1st/2nd stage, no original consolidation labelling)
- EX: 2024-08-27 NQ 1m: ORG with prior settlement above open; sweep of relative equal lows into a first FVG low 19,455.25, reversal, then rally through reclaimed FVG and breaker to ORG high (@57:22, @59:27, @79:32)
