### 003 | 20240807 | Lecture 3 - NDOG/NWOG, engineering liquidity, afternoon session, holding trades
tags: NDOG/NWOG (P5), consequent encroachment, EQH/EQL (L4), SMT n/a, FVG (P1), bearish OB (P6), turtle soup n/a, Judas swing (T2), killzones, Silver Bullet
- DEF: NDOG = gap between the 16:59 NY close (last 1m print before the 17:00 halt) and the 18:00 NY first print of the next session; marked Mon-Thu. Three levels per gap: the higher of (close, open), the lower of them, and the midpoint = consequent encroachment (CE). A gap formed Tuesday 18:00 is a reference for WEDNESDAY's trading (@17:42, @21:20, @53:27)
- DEF: NWOG = Friday 16:59 close vs Sunday 18:00 open; same three levels; tagged "NW" and dated on the chart (@28:34)
- RULE: Keep the last 5 NDOGs (5 TRADING days, holidays skip) and last 5 NWOGs (5 weeks) on a dedicated layout, rays extended right, each labelled with date; older ones can still matter if inside the narrative / within ~150 handles of price, but 5/5 is the rule-based default look-back (@23:54, @28:34, @35:49, @26:31)
- RULE: A gap stays relevant even after price touches its high, CE or low; it is not "filled = dead". Touched only once = most sensitive ("hot") (@35:18, @48:46)
- RULE: Divide the gap into quarters (0/25/50/75/100%); price reacts at the quadrant levels (upper quadrant, CE, lower quadrant); example NQ gap showed stops/turns on 0.25/0.75 levels to the tick (@31:40, @32:10, @34:17)
- RULE: CE of any gap/FVG: for a down-close FVG the best shorts form in the LOWER half (upper half must stay unvisited); for an up-close FVG best longs in the UPPER half. If price fails to even reach CE of the gap on a retest, it signals strength of the opposite side (@33:46, @104:32, @226:06)
- RULE: Cluster logic: if several NDOG/NWOG sit on one side of price (esp. 3 of last 5 on one side), expect price to be drawn to that side; use as filter for which side to trade, not a hard rule (@38:27, @43:02, @92:16)
- CTX: 7:00, 8:00, 9:00 NY: watch for the first 30 min after each hour to deliver the move opposite to the one the prior hour set up; at/after 07:00 note relative equal highs/lows that form and the NDOG/NWOG on the far side; the EQH side is expected to be run first (Judas) when the opening-gap magnet lies the other way (@15:39, @51:21, @53:54, @106:45)
- DEF: Filter for a "high-probability" relative equal LOW: two swing lows, the right-hand (later) one HIGHER than the left; mirror for equal highs (right one lower) (@88:36)
- RULE: Priority of draws: (1) NDOG/NWOG, (2) relative equal highs/lows (real resting orders) and session extremes, (3) inefficiencies/FVG, subordinate (@114:00, @115:34)
- CTX: News filter: if the morning (08:30/10:00 releases) has no medium/high impact data, morning is likely a rangy false-breakout day; wait for the afternoon. Clean afternoon window ~13:30-14:30; Silver Bullet 14:00-15:00, then 15:00 hour to close. Bond auction 13:00 = medium-impact driver (@04:52, @79:53, @80:23, @86:34, @87:34)
- MMXM: Entry template (short): narrative = down draw to NDOG + London lows / EQL; 12:42 up-close run, fair value gap rounding over with bodies respecting it; price rallies into a bearish OB / FVG that is not touched at CE; short at the OB low, stop above the CE of the lower FVG; wait for displacement and "immediate rebalance"; a 2nd FVG above is anticipated but not required (@94:16, @105:37, @109:52, @208:20, @213:22)
- RULE: If price tags CE of the FVG before reversing, skip that entry and wait for a retest higher inside the upper half of the gap; stop then goes above that CE zone (@104:32, @211:21)
- RULE: Position handling (demo): 6 contracts at the first entry, 4 more pyramided at a higher price (4 up-trade scale-in); stop to first entry minus 2 ticks once price fails to reach CE of the FVG; partial/limit order below London low; full scale-out well below (@110:22, @216:00, @119:08)
- DEF: Scale of runs for NQ: scalp <50 handles; intermediate >=50 handles; large swing >=100 handles. Holding longer needs a DIFFERENT, larger reason (HTF draw), not just a 1m entry; train by taking a partial then tape-reading the rest (@71:45, @85:01, @100:57)
- EX: 20240807 NQ 1m demo short: entry ~18321.25 below bearish OB, ~18291.25 add, exit ~18230; >100 handles, took out London low and moved toward NDOG below (@107:19, @119:08)

### 004 | 20240808 | Lecture 4 - walk-forward drill: 08:30 spool toward NDOG/NWOG clusters

- RULE: Per-day gap workflow: NDOG levels = higher of (17:00 close, 18:00 open) = "high", lower = "low", 50% = CE. - RULE: Directional bias at the 08:30 spool: look at where the CLUSTER of NDOGs (last 5 days) + NWOG sits relative to price just before the release; price is drawn toward the side with the stack, even gaps already touched/crossed. - CTX: 08:30 is the key time whether or not news is scheduled; medium/high impact data only enlarges magnitude and speed of the move ("spooling"). Report values are irrelevant. Sitting between two gaps pre-release = no trade, wait (@09:44, @21:34, @65:39, @87:34)
- CTX: Macro window = last 10 min of an hour through first 10 min of next hour (xx:50-xx:10); price spools there to take liquidity or fill an inefficiency (@23:39, @81:18)
- RULE: Entry logic before the 8:30 print: only if price first dropped into a clean FVG/OB in the discount of the recent range (below 50% of the latest down leg) just before 08:30 and the draw is above -> buy the FVG into the news; otherwise wait and use a 15s chart afterward to trade first OB/FVG/volume imbalance supporting the draw (@57:48, @95:10, @108:28)

- RULE: "Institutional order entry drill": short-term short from a FVG that is not filled to CE; keep the other half open (breakaway-gap logic); expect 15-20+ handles; stop above the gap; partial at the level reached first (@92:35, @93:06)
- DEF: Bodies vs wicks: candle BODIES respect a PD array (NDOG CE, FVG edge), wicks overshoot slightly; a body closing through marks failure. A reclaimed FVG (re-entering a previously offered FVG) is tradable again (@19:02, @90:02, @91:33)
- DEF: Balanced price range here = both-side delivered overlap of an up-close and down-close gap (seller delivery + buyer delivery within the same price span); no need for price to explore deeper into it (@83:21, @117:19)
- DEF: Bearish breaker = high-low-higher-high that sweeps buy-side; the FVG inside is the one to watch, not the final down candles; that FVG may later invert (IFVG) as an upside objective (@31:18, @61:31, @82:21)
- RULE: Fib use = range midpoint only: latest large directional leg; EQ (50%) above = premium, below = discount; no oscillators/divergence (@77:07, @147:07)
- CTX: SMT used only as qualifier: NQ/ES/YM on 1-5m "matrix"; one failing to confirm adds speed/quality; not necessary (@128:13)
- RULE: Real stop always, no mental stops; hide P/L; first month = log only, no demo entries (@62:35, @104:23)

### 005 | 20240809 | Lecture 5 - Asia session (18:00-21:00) protocol, initial BSL/SSL, measured-move targets, opening range, gap midpoint, Friday retracement

- DEF: Turtle Soup = ICT's reversal version of the Turtle 20-day breakout: a raid beyond a prior N-day extreme that fails and returns inside the range is a fade; he says it is not only 20-day high/low and promises added characteristics later (not delivered in this file) (@08:20, @09:23)
- RULE: "Initial liquidity" protocol after the 18:00 open (Asia): (1) mark NDOG (CE; quadrants only if gap >20 handles on NQ, else eyeball); (2) price must LEAVE the gap; (3) mark first short-term high and short-term low printed after 18:00 as initial BSL / initial SSL; (4) price takes one short-term extreme, crosses back over the NDOG and takes the opposite short-term extreme; (5) at 19:00 (Asia key time, window 19:00-21:00 NY) expect the algorithm to run the side it left unpierced. If nothing breaks either side by 19:00, stand aside (dull day) (@36:13, @41:29, @45:06, @47:09, @49:39, @82:30)
- MMXM: Asia long template: at 19:00 candle price dips back to touch the NDOG high after displacement; if displacement left a buy-side-imbalance/sell-side-inefficiency (up-close FVG/volume imbalance) and price does NOT return into it (breakaway gap), buy: entry = limit inside discount part of the last down-close candle (below its body/close, OB wick) e.g. 18559.75 vs close 18560; stop below NDOG CE (move to breakeven or at least NDOG top once the short-term high is taken); two qualifiers = displacement without retracing into imbalance, then sweep of initial BSL (@47:39, @50:40, @53:14, @54:15, @56:20)

## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules (<=10)
  1. NDOG = 16:59 close vs 18:00 open (Mon-Thu); NWOG = Fri 16:59 vs Sun 18:00; levels = high, low, CE; quadrants at 25/75% only if gap >~20 NQ handles; list the last 5 trading days of NDOGs and last 5 weeks of NWOGs; a gap counts as the reference for the NEXT trading day (003 @21:20, @28:34; 004 @13:22; 005 @31:27).
  2. Gap cluster logic: count of NDOGs/NWOG above vs below price just before 07:00/08:00/08:30/09:00 chooses the likely draw; price sandwiched between two gaps = no trade (003 @38:27; 004 @51:21).
  3. Draw priority: NDOG/NWOG > relative equal highs/lows and session extremes > FVG/inefficiencies (003 @114:00).
  4. EQL/EQH quality filter: right-hand swing must be higher (lows) / lower (highs) than the left; very "clean" equal highs are expected to be run (003 @88:36; 005 @88:13).
  5. FVG CE test: in a retest, failure to reach CE = weakness for the side tested; sell from the lower half of a down-close FVG, buy upper half of an up-close FVG; unvisited half stays open = breakaway (003 @33:46, @104:32; 004 @92:35; 005 @17:05).
  6. Asia protocol: after 18:00, mark NDOG; initial BSL/SSL = first short-term high/low after the open; price should cross the gap and take one side then the other, then run at 19:00 (005 @36:13-@49:39).
  7. Long entry from displacement at 19:00: limit in discount of the last down-close candle, stop below NDOG CE; two qualifiers (no return into imbalance, initial BSL taken); targets = measured move -1.0 to -1.5 midpoint (005 @53:14, @60:26).
  8. 08:30 is a key time even without news; news only scales the move; pre-news entry only from a clean FVG/OB in discount toward the cluster (004 @57:48, @87:34).
  9. Friday expectation: one-sided week retraces 20-30% (max ~40%) of weekly range; RTH gap-down returns to mid-gap ~70% (005 @97:58, @86:08).
  10. Size ladder (NQ): <50 handles scout, >=50 intermediate, >=100 swing; position scaling 6 + 4 contracts, stop to first entry -2 ticks (003 @71:45, @110:53, @111:23).
- Definition changes vs DRAFT
  - NDOG/NWOG (P5): confirmed 17:00 close to 18:00 open; the "close" is the 16:59 print of the last 1m bar; DRAFT said Fri close - Sun open for NWOG = confirmed. New: 5-day / 5-week life cycle and rays labelled with dates; levels are H, L, CE, and optional quadrants.
  - CE (consequent encroachment) = midpoint of a GAP/FVG; "equilibrium" reserved for a delivered range (003 @60:09). DRAFT used CE for both.

  - Day boundary for "true day" = midnight NY (003 @17:11); session reference opening gap = 18:00 (NDOG).

  - FVG in OB: orders = bodies respect, wicks overshoot (004 @19:02).
  - NQ handle scale: 10 handles = scalp scale, 50+ intermediate, 100+ swing (003).
