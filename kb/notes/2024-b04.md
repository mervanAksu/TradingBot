- DEF: A wick on any swing high/low is treated like a gap: range = body edge (open or close, whichever is nearer the wick) to the extreme tip; its midpoint is its consequent encroachment (CE), not a "mean threshold" (that term is reserved for OB bodies). - RULE: Wick-CE read when the wick lies ABOVE price in a bearish context: price trades up into the CE and bodies fail to close above it = weakness, expect a lower high on the next candle and a drop; a wick may pierce CE, bodies must not hold above it (@30:46, @31:16, @54:53)
- RULE: Confluence stack worth waiting for on a retracement: 18:00 open price + FVG (or its CE) + wick CE (+ prior candle high). Highest-probability stop of the retracement = the first real level reached in that stack, before the FVG top (@22:25, @77:30, @111:54)

- DEF: Swing high ("ring high") = candle whose high is above the highs of the candles on both sides; swing highs that keep forming below an older cluster mean an intermediate-term run is underway (@39:27)
- DEF: Bearish OB = the WHOLE run of consecutive up-closed candles before the drop, not only the last one; zone = bodies (wicks are "colouring outside the lines"); mean threshold = midpoint of those bodies.
