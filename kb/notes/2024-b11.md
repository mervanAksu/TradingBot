### 036 | 20240927 | High Resistance Liquidity Conditions (live NQ, Friday)
tags: opening range gap (ORG), first presented FVG (1m, >=9:31), high/low resistance liquidity run (L5/L6), TGIF weekly retracement, quadrants/CE, turtle soup (L7), Judas, macros (T5), partials, SMT-free
- DEF: Opening range gap (ORG) = prior RTH settlement (16:14/16:15 NY close print) to the 9:30 RTH open print; it must be viewed on RTH data (ETH charts hide it). Opening range = 9:30-10:00. Treat the whole ORG as one inefficiency: use its CE (50%) and quadrant levels (25/75) (@05:15, @05:45, @10:10, @105:35)
- RULE: ORG fill statistic: ~70% of the time half of the ORG (its CE) is traded to inside 9:30-10:00; gap up -> initial bias down, gap down -> bias up. The 9:30 open price is a static anchor, so a brief extension beyond it first does not cancel the draw (@05:15, @81:00, @84:14, @151:00)
- RULE: First presented FVG = first 1-min FVG whose 3 candles start at 9:31 or later (9:30 candle is excluded because of gap volatility); it is the one reference for the day; above it = bullish, below = bearish, volatility in first 30 min is allowed to cross it (@03:44, @07:19, @38:52, @40:23)
- RULE: ORG size gauge: want >= ~40 handles, ICT prefers ~75; < 75 handles -> expect sluggish morning (high-resistance), < 40 -> definitely lackluster; a big gap = precursor of fluid delivery (@07:52, @92:52, @93:23)
- RULE: Bearish bias inside ORG: no candle BODIES in the upper half (above the 75% level) of the ORG; wicks into it are fine. A wick-only poke leaving the top portion unfilled is the "tell" that the lower half will be hit. Mirror for bullish: keep lower half of a bullish FVG open (@22:00, @23:02, @104:35, @105:05)
- RULE: Entry template (short, gap-up day): wait for displacement below the first 1m FVG with a body close away from it, then a return to the FVG (touch of its low / CE) -> short; drop to 15s and use the first 15s FVG formed after displacement; do not short inside the FVG merely because it is tagged (@26:04, @26:35, @27:05, @106:36)
- RULE: Friday (TGIF) rule: after an up week, expect a 20-30% retracement of the week's range (high to low of the week, fib levels 0.2 and 0.3 measured down from the weekly high); bearish week mirrored upward. If not done Friday, Monday often does it. 20% level = first partial/target, 30% = next (@10:41, @23:32, @80:21, @84:14, @88:16)
- RULE: Draw = relative equal lows (obvious sell-side) below; stack at least 3 factors (time + liquidity/inefficiency + PD array/day-of-week) before an idea; time element = macro windows xx:50-xx:10 (e.g. 9:50-10:10), described as 10 min then 20 min intervals; macros mean "price will run", not direction - direction comes from gap/bias (@13:53, @14:23, @28:07, @52:07)
- DEF: High-resistance liquidity run (HRLR as a MARKET CONDITION, not a pool): price hesitates to reach obvious liquidity; signatures = small opening gap, price spends many candles inside / re-enters the same FVG several times, spiky runs outside FVGs, loss of tick precision at PD arrays. Low-resistance: FVG is touched then price leaves immediately, delivery to the tick, fast targets (@93:23, @94:45, @193:00, @217:00, @263:36, @290:06)
- RULE: Codable proxy for HRLR day: count candles-in-FVG and number of re-entries of the first-presented 1m/15s FVG (>1 revisit or multiple closes inside) and ORG < 75 handles -> flag high-resistance; inefficiency should be "toe in water, then repelled" (@101:00, @131:40, @264:36, @271:00)
- RULE: In HRLR conditions: stop must be at least ~2x the FVG height measured from entry (beyond the far side of the FVG, not just above it); use micros/min size; on LRLR days CE-based tight stop (one tick beyond CE) is fine (@107:07, @108:09, @109:11)
- RULE: Partials: take partial each time price takes out a new low below entry (bearish); on HRLR day ICT/student said ~6 of 10 contracts; if gaps are being chewed, cut to 1 contract; max leverage only on LRLR days (@111:15, @112:17, @115:50, @129:07)
- MMXM: HRLR day structure = repeated turtle soups: run buy stops above a short-term/equal high, sell off, repeat; each small run takes trailed stops while the manipulator builds the short; "Judas" = the early run into upper ORG half (not above open) that traps both breakout longs and short stops (@104:35, @121:26, @132:40, @133:43, @265:00)
- CTX: Don't confuse HRLR day with seek-and-destroy; two S&D days never occur in one week; HRLR days are mostly manual-intervention (precision lost), algorithmic precision resumes after manipulation; FOMC/NFP/CPI pre-release sideways is separate (no trade) (@91:20, @135:13, @226:00)
- EX: 2024-09-27 NQ 1m RTH: gap up (open ~20,421), first 1m FVG at 9:34, relative equal lows below as draw; price chopped inside the FVG, ran upper ORG quadrant at ~10:00 macro, then sold off through the equal lows; weekly 20% / 30% levels (~4:21 handle area) used as partials (@80:51, @83:11, @168:00)
