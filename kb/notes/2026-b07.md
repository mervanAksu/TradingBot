## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules
  1. RTH opening range gap = prior 16:14-16:15 ET settlement vs 9:30 open; its CE is the first-30-60-minute draw (claimed ~70%); gap above/below decides buy- vs sell-side bias (107/109).
  2. Lunch macro: first-hour dealing range 9:30-10:30; macro window 10:50-11:10 (lunch session 11:30-13:30); default = retrace to sell-side lows formed after ~10:00 (104).
  3. Turtle-soup short inside the macro: minor buy-side taken, up-close candle's gap flips to bearish IFVG on a body close through; enter retest of lower half, partials at lows, final at the origin wick CE (104).
  4. Body rule: bearish = bodies stay out of the upper half of a gap/wick/PD array (wick pokes allowed); bullish = bodies stay out of the lower half (103-109).
  5. Wick-as-FVG: a wick left of a gap is the first-presented FVG; use CE; failure to reach the prior candle's open/close = heaviness (104, 106-108).
  6. TGIF: 20-30% (max 40%) weekly-range retrace Thursday 13:00 to Friday close; if missed, filled Sunday/Monday open (105).
  7. "To it and through it": EQH raid after a sell-side clear must expand immediately; a shallow poke then return = false breakout; target = half the stop-hunt leg projected above (103).
  8. Drill model: fill in a 1m FVG, stop at gap CE/far side, 1:1 for 10-15 handles on MNQ; walk away if no gap (103).
  9. One-sidedness filter and decoupling rule: skip when NQ/ES/YM disagree or both sides arguable; short the weakest index (105, 106, 108).
  10. 8:30 news high/low is a draw; use continuous contract + front month; toggle settings to expose volume imbalance (105, 107).
