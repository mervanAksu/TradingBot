## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules:
  1. NWOG: edges = Fri 16:59 close and Sun 18:00 open (1m/5m, not daily); CE 50%, quarters 25/75; keep last 4-5; orbiting it = consolidation, leaving without return = expansion (039/030).
  2. Two NWOG variants: actual (Fri close - Sun open) and RTH-based (Fri close - Mon 09:30 open) (039@14:36).
  3. FVG quarter map: bullish gap should hold in upper quarter (high to CE); close through CE = failure; entries at high, high-CE midpoint, CE; stop below gap low; BISI = bullish, SIBI = bearish (030@19:23, @58:24).
  4. Wick CE: failure to reach wick CE (or ~25% of wick) after a bearish read = strong weakness; stubby wick tends to be probed, long wick reaches CE (028@16:03, @14:31).
  5. Opening range = 09:30-10:00; opening gap vs 16:15 RTH close; unfilled gap attempt in first 30 min = weakness (030@89:00; 043@08:30).
  6. Macros: 09:50-10:10 morning, last hour 15:15-15:45 (030), 14:50-15:10 afternoon (043); runs to buy/sell side from an MSS (030@22:31).
  7. SD projections (-1/-2/-3) from the leg low-high that preceded retracement; targets coincide with CE of FVG and liquidity (029@36:33, 043@12:37).
  8. Breaker = highest up-close (or last down-close) candle body in the leg taken, not wicks; bullish breaker + BISI is the long (029@22:26).
  9. Midnight open: shorts above, longs below (043@15:08).
  10. No trading before high-impact news (CPI/NFP/FOMC); high-resistance conditions produce chop and one-tick misses (028, 029, 030).
