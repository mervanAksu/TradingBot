### 142 | 20260102 | 2026 SMC Lecture - buy-side-of-curve FVG, Model 2022 + turtle soup, RTH ORG half-gap short, wick quadrants (Fri Jan 2 2026, NQ)

- DEF: "Buy side of the curve" imbalance = an FVG/volume imbalance sitting on the LEFT (rising) side of a swing high, before the turn; it is preferred over any FVG on the right (falling) side as the premium array to short from, because it is more precise/sensitive; it behaves as an inversion FVG (zone = candle-high-to-wick-high plus the body-to-body volume imbalance) (@06:12, @09:18, @14:22)
- RULE: Preference rule: if an inefficiency exists on the buy-side-of-curve of the swing high, use it over right-side FVGs; cost = no fill if price turns from a smaller, lower gap first (@10:19, @19:00)
- DEF: Model 2022 restated as: short-term high, then a higher short-term high that fails (turtle soup / false breakout, no complete reversal needed), pullback into range; the FIRST FVG formed after that failure is the entry gap; new twist: keep and extend that FPFVG forward in time for later use (@07:45, @08:15)
- RULE: Short entry inside the FPFVG: wanted behaviour = bodies stay below the gap's CE; wicks through are tolerated ("mohawk" = wick-only excursions, bodies stay inside); wanted then displacement THROUGH the gap's low with no bodies back up into it - no retest of an old low = heaviness/strong continuation (@31:38, @32:08, @36:45)
- RULE: Add-on entry: a retest wick into the lower quadrant (or low) of the FPFVG after it flips to IFVG would have been an add; stopping short (gap between wick and gap low) = no add (@34:11)
- RULE: Opening range gap method (taught to students): bias from daily, target = half (CE) of the RTH opening range gap (settle to 09:30 open), ~70% filled by 10:00; aim a few ticks past CE, take partials before it; shorts still valid after 10:00 if HTF is bearish (@20:18, @22:21, @33:10, @73:00)
- RULE: Partial/stop management: take a partial when price is within ~10-15 handles of target after a straight run; then roll the stop: each new swing low taken -> lower stop to ~3 handles above price; never leave the stop stale after a new equity extreme; with partials taken the stop may be looser, but tighten when PD arrays overlap in volatile markets (@39:19, @40:21, @41:22)
- DEF: Wick grading: the wick of the key daily swing candle is a gap too; use the candle body-open as the first range, then once price trades through the open use the wick; grade it as high / upper quadrant / CE / lower quadrant / low; the daily wick lower-quadrant (25,267.5) was the exact turn (@12:19, @13:20, @52:31)
- RULE: Relative equal H/L candidate: the later (right-hand) low is higher than the anchor low (or later high lower) -> high-probability sweep candidate; sweep done by wick, bodies stay above/below level (@25:58, @45:24, @46:26)
- MMXM: Daily-chart reversal off the suspension-block lower quadrant (a wick quadrant): sell side swept (relative equal low, wicks only), then the last down-close candle becomes an order block once price closes above it; price retraces to its mean threshold, rallies into an FVG = "smart money reversal", labelled low-risk buy and "consolidation / reaccumulation, second-stage reaccumulation", then the run clears the prior high - the market maker buy model completed off a quadrant on a wick (@45:24, @46:57, @47:29)

## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules (<=10)
  1. Frame time first: Asia session 19:00-05:00 and London 02:00-11:00 are whole-session ranges (not just killzones); find smooth/relative-equal highs/lows inside them and treat 07:00-09:00 as the run to that pool (140 @41:12, @52:35)
  2. FPFVG = first FVG on displacement through a short-term high/low at window start (07:00 pre-market, 09:30 open, or right after a turtle soup); NQ gaps < ~10 handles probably not it; project it 20 trading days (140 @67:21, 143 @13:26)
  3. Relative equal H/L: the later point must be slightly inside (right-hand low higher / high lower) -> high-probability sweep; graded wick of the anchor gives CE/quadrant targets and the partial level (140 @79:06, 143 @24:09)
  4. RTH opening range gap (prior 16:00 settle vs 09:30 open): CE reached before 10:00 about 70% of time; aim just beyond CE and partial earlier (140 @129:38, 142 @22:21)
  5. Model 2022 = failed higher short-term high (turtle soup) then first FVG after; prefer an imbalance on the buy side of the curve (left of swing high) over right-side FVGs (142 @07:45, @10:19)
  6. Partial near target (10-15 handles away) and ratchet stop at each new swing/equity extreme (~3 handles room) (142 @39:19, @40:21)
  7. Quadrant/eighth grading (high, UQ, CE, LQ, low) on every gap and key wicks; turns often occur at the LQ/UQ of a wick (142 @12:49, @52:31)
  8. Draw-on-liquidity gate: no clear draw = no trade; avoid thin/holiday periods, lunch window 11:30-13:30, first Feb Tuesday as start-of-year risk date (143 @09:19, @40:31, 140 @71:59)
