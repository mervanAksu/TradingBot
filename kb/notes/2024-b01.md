### 001 | 20240805 | 2024 Mentorship L1 - mindset + "smooth vs jagged" bias frame
tags: EQH/EQL (L4), draw on liquidity, FVG (P1), breaker (P7), order block (P6), volume imbalance (P4), displacement (S5), premium/discount (S3), time windows (T1), Judas swing, priming
- RULE: Bias = whichever side holds a clean "smooth" relative-equal pool formed before the window starts. Scan three windows per morning, 07:00-07:30, 08:00-08:30, 09:00-09:30 NY; pools must have formed after 07:00 for the first window (London overlap is tolerated, not required to be cleared). Use only 15m (once, as map), 5m and 1m (@103:36, @104:07, @125:02, @125:33)
- RULE: If both an equal-high pool and an equal-low pool exist, do nothing until one is traded through; the untouched side becomes the draw. Sitting out is acceptable, no obligation to trade every day (@113:24, @129:17)
- RULE: Probability filter for equal highs: of two close swing highs, the left one slightly higher than the right one = high-odds target (mirror for lows: right low slightly higher than left). Tolerance is deliberately not disclosed; "not much" difference (@75:34, @77:41, @78:45)
- DEF: "Smooth" = flat, overlapping, low-wick/unvisited stretch with clean equal swings; "jagged" = area already traded through with many sharp swings = work already done, no longer a magnet. Price goes to smooth areas, moves away from jagged ones (@69:59, @121:59, @134:26)
- DEF: Pre-market range = the 30 min from 07:00 / 08:00 / 09:00; opening range = the following 30 min (07:30-08:00 after the 07:00 pre-range; 09:30-10:00 after the 09:00 pre-range). No 15-minute opening range, 30 min only (@111:53, @112:23)
- DEF: Draw on liquidity is a working first target (old high/low or inefficiency), not a final terminus; retracements and a lower low are allowed before it is reached (@66:55)
- DEF: Fair value gap polarity naming used here: down-close FVG = sell-side imbalance / sibi, up-close = buy-side imbalance / bisi (@61:42)
- DEF: Bullish breaker described as the whole run of consecutive up-close candles before the drop (range), but the most sensitive part is the final up-close candle; body, not wick, matters (@83:20, @83:52, @84:23)
- RULE: Entry anchors cited in the live example: (a) bullish breaker after sell-side sweep, (b) a premium/discount check with a fib from just above the target pool to the equal lows (not the extreme low = manipulation); FVG/gap near the 50% line was used, and a volume imbalance must be included in the FVG body (@83:20, @120:28, @121:28, @164:25)
- MMXM: Sequence shown: smooth pool above -> early low sweep under relative equal lows (jagged zone) -> displacement above short-term high leaving down-close candle (order block) -> retrace into breaker/OB and FVG in discount -> run to equal highs. Second-leg "redistribution" mentioned after inversion FVG (@52:15, @53:24, @124:30)
- CTX: Algorithmic delivery is time-driven; macros are ~20-minute windows; 08:30 starts a spool for liquidity/inefficiency. Without the correct time of day no pattern works (@13:58, @14:29, @53:55, @100:32)
- CTX: Counter-move at 09:30 inside the opening range is a Judas swing, opposite to the real move set by the smooth highs (@123:30)
- CTX: Manual intervention (FOMC, CPI, NFP, geopolitical shock) breaks the model and is untradeable; do not chase; never over-leverage (@54:58, @127:14)
- CTX: Day starts with 07:00 as AM session start; 08:00-08:30 plus 09:00-09:30 chances stack: a pool untouched by earlier window gets more likely, not less (@125:33, @197:37)
- EX: 2024-08-05 NQ 1m/5m/15m: equal highs at 17820.75 formed ~07:00; early weak low sweep post-08:30, up-close breaker, then price ran to the pool; 55 -> 596 retrace scalp noted; ~09:30 drop = Judas into breaker then rally to target (@51:45, @83:20, @166:26, @226:00)

### 002 | 20240806 | 2024 Mentorship L2 - rules for the 07:00 hunt, FVG classification, breaker / inversion sequence, journaling
tags: EQH/EQL (L4), FVG (P1), IFVG (P2), volume imbalance (P4), breaker (P7), OTE (S4), MSS (S1), Judas swing/AMD (T2), London range, immediate rebalance, 15s chart, std-dev projection
- RULE: Chart set = 15m (upper left), 5m, 1m, optional 15s; nothing above 15m. Draw vertical lines at 07:00, 08:00, 09:00 NY. Only show London (02:00-05:00 range) if the morning session reaches its high or low (@23:04, @60:04, @92:30, @104:27, @201:00)
- RULE: Hunt for equal highs/lows only from swings formed after 07:00 (or after 08:00 / 09:00 for later windows; at 08:00 and 09:00 earlier post-07:00 swings count, pre-07:00 do not). Pre-07:00 pools are ignored by beginners; London-profile traders may use them. Do not mark a pool before it exists on the chart; no pre-emptive annotation (@94:34, @103:14, @105:15, @190:00)
- RULE: First 30 min after each hour start (07:00, 08:00, 09:00) is expected to produce a counter-move (Judas) against the pool's side: equal highs above -> expect a dip first; equal lows below -> expect a pop first. If it does not appear, wait for more information or a deeper retracement (@180:00, @214:27, @216:00)
- RULE: Same-direction pools only: when both an equal-high pool and equal-low pool exist, wait for one side to be taken, then the other side is the draw (@204:00)
- RULE: Once a direction is chosen, keep only FVGs that agree. Bearish narrative: extend every sell-side-imbalance-buy-side-inefficiency (down-close big candle, gap between prior low and next high) to the right and watch for price to return into it from below; ignore opposite-colour FVGs unless price violently reverses them (then they become IFVG) (@165:00, @173:24, @176:00, @191:00, @225:52)
- RULE: FVG precision: if the gap has a volume imbalance (candle bodies do not overlap or touch), wicks poking into the gap are noise as long as the wick stays at or below the consequent encroachment (50%); treat the body-to-body zone as the real FVG. Keep the original full-gap levels (low, CE, high) even after partial fills (@67:03, @69:33, @70:04, @125:30, @159:16)
- RULE: Price often overshoots a limit level by about a spread/tick; not getting filled is acceptable, "do not be a dick for a tick" (@34:09, @70:34)
- RULE: Breaker stop: bearish breaker = last down-close candle (full run of consecutive down-close candles), stop 1-2 ticks above the highest high of that run; if an FVG sits inside the stop-hunt leg, price may spike above with the wick but bodies stay inside or below that gap (@88:52, @89:22)
- RULE: Entry preference ladder after a stop hunt reversal: first FVG formed in the leg before the stop run (the change-in-state FVG, treated like an order block) > breaker candle if that FVG is absent. Do not default to the low-hanging breaker only; check the FVG at the premium first (@88:21, @80:11, @163:17)
- RULE: Skip when price has already come back into a zone twice (overlapped and respected more than once); third touch = low probability (@106:00)
- RULE: Immediate rebalance: expansion candle/range followed straight away by a drop that only touches that candle's high (or low), then proceeds to the target; an FVG left open under it acts like a breakaway gap (@147:37, @299:00)
- RULE: OTE reference restated: after MSS, measure swing high to low, entry zone 62-72% retracement, 70.5% sweet spot (@96:38, @97:09)
- RULE: Range-projection drill (post-hoc): anchor a fib on the 07:00 / 08:00 / 09:00 window range (high to lowest low since window start); use 1, 2, 2.5 standard deviations as ballpark extension for how far a run can go; only meaningful together with real levels (old lows/highs), never alone (@217:40, @220:41, @223:19)
- DEF: Down-close FVG = sell-side imbalance / buy-side inefficiency (SIBI); up-close FVG = BISI. Same naming as L1, spelled out as: SIBI offered delivery on the downside, leaves buy-side inefficiency to be revisited from below (@63:57, @76:39)
- DEF: Inversion FVG: an FVG that price runs through and then comes back to; highest-precision ones are those that formed after the 07:00 hunt, after stops above/below were taken; all PD arrays have an inversion role (@74:06, @143:07, @200:00)

- DEF: Morning session = 07:00-11:00 NY (technically to 12:00), ICT prefers done by ~10:15-10:30; AM-session first characteristic = retrace into London range (02:00-05:00 high-low) (@151:09, @190:15)

## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules
  1. Window scan: 07:00/08:00/09:00 NY; only equal highs/lows that formed after 07:00 count; one pool untouched, if both, wait for first to be taken (001@125:02, 002@94:34, 002@204:00)
  2. Equal-high quality: left swing slightly higher than right = prime target; equal lows: right low slightly higher (001@75:34, 002@73:36)
  3. First 30 min of each window = expected counter-move (Judas) opposite the pool; if absent, wait (002@180:00, 002@214:27)
  4. Only trade FVGs aligned with direction; extend each to the right; enter on a return from the pre-hunt FVG, fall back to breaker (002@88:21, 002@165:00)
  5. FVG must include volume imbalance (body gap); wicks into it up to CE are ignored (002@67:03, 002@70:04)
  6. Bearish breaker stop = 1-2 ticks above the high of the full consecutive down-close run (002@89:22)
  7. Skip a zone on 3rd touch; skip when pools are not obvious (002@106:00, 002@254:00)
  8. OTE 62-72% (70.5) after MSS; 1, 2, 2.5 SD fib projection of 07:00/08:00/09:00 window range for target ballpark (002@97:09, 002@217:40)
  9. Morning session 07:00-11:00; 15m/5m/1m only; one trade/day, one contract while learning (001@104:07, 002@23:04)
  10. Immediate rebalance: expansion candle then retest of its high only, then run to pool (002@147:37)
