## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules:
  1. Platform time = NY; day anchor = 00:00 NY open (not the platform daily open); buy at/below it when bullish.
  2. FVG sequencing: first return to CE, second return to full fill = balanced range; a FVG left open on breakaway is a continuation signature.
  3. BISI/SIBI = single-candle FVG (up/down). Liquidity void reserved for true gap; volume imbalance = body-to-body gap with only wicks.
  4. Entry stack: sell-side taken at 09:30 -> price returns into bullish breaker/OB with FVG inside ("unicorn") -> buy at CE/mean threshold, stop below MT.
  5. Institutional order flow entry drill: dip into an open FVG short of 50% or exactly one tick under its edge -> add.
  6. NFP/FOMC: avoid 08:30 and later in week; trade Mon-Wed AM; imprecision of a few ticks around news.
  7. Last-hour (15:00-16:00) PM play: run to old high/EQH/imbalance; setup must exist by ~15:50 or no trade; AM 08:30-11:00 / PM 14:00-16:00.
  8. Scaled exits: 5-handle units, one contract per new high ("run down equity"), final target a daily volume imbalance or prior close.
  9. Day-range timing: LOD (if bullish) forms in London killzone, HOD between 10:00 and 12:00 NY; macro 09:50-10:10 can force a liquidity run.
  10. SMT: compare ES/MES and NQ/ES; choose stronger instrument; relative strength by cleaner FVG/displacement.
- Definition changes vs DRAFT:
  - Daily open: 00:00 NY, not 18:00 (L8/T2).
  - Mean threshold (50% of OB/breaker) vs consequent encroachment (50% of FVG): different names, same midpoint (P1/P6).
  - Liquidity void (P11) stricter than DRAFT: true gap only, BISI/SIBI is a single-candle FVG.
  - Breaker defined by swing sequence: high-low-higher high (bearish) / low-high-lower low (bullish) with the last series candle as the block; 'reclaimed OB' = OB retested after being left.
  - Volume imbalance (P4) = bodies don't overlap, wicks do; mean daily-chart use as target/support.
  - Killzones: NY 07:00-09:00 (Forex lecture) vs CLAUDE.md 07:00-10:00; London close 10:00-12:00.
  - Standard deviation 1.5-3.5 projections from London range (T3) rather than only CBDR.
