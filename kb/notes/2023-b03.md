## Batch summary
- Top mechanical rules (<=10):
  1. Bias gate: DXY higher = risk-off (ES/FX shorts, no easy longs); lower = risk-on; if DXY and ES setups disagree, shrink size or stand aside (022 @03:42, @98:35).

  3. 2022 model sequence: buy-side (EQH/old high) taken -> displacement closes below a short-term low (one-tick pierce acceptable) -> mark that leg -> enter on retrace into FVG/VI/OB inside it, above 50% of the leg; stop above MT of the up-close OB; no chasing (022 @36:20, @57:15, @59:15; 026 @91:03).
  4. Pyramiding/entry cut-off: add only in premium half of the leg; stop adding once price crosses the midpoint of the discount half; size by stack (6/3/1) with larger size at the first array (022 @49:36; 025 @47:39; 026 @103:50).
  5. Entry price in an FVG = CE (highest probability) or the near edge/1 tick beyond (OFE drill); min potential target about 10 handles though 5 handles is first objective (024 @23:19, @25:23).
  6. Time filters: 2-3 min on 1m after entry in chop (022), about 10 min (025 @17:19, 027 @24:53); no new shorts late-day against the afternoon reversal; no fade of afternoon reversals within ~10-15 min of 16:00 (026 @120:12).

  8. Macros: 10:50-11:10 run for untapped liquidity (entry on close-proximity FVG/VI/last down-close candle after a shift, 15s-1m), noon/13:00 reversion to AM stops, last hour 15:00-16:00 with ~15:45-15:50 run (022 @185:40; 025 @53:16; 026 @115:37; 027 @40:28).

  10. Stand-aside rule for HRLR: if the easy model already ran to target pre-09:30, expect chop at the 09:30 open and trade the PM (022 @75:38, @133:23).
