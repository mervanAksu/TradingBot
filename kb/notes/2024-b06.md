### 012 | 20240819 | Holding the daily-range trade: opening-price buy, lunch macro, last-hour macros
tags: power of 3 (T2), midnight/9:30 open (L8), NWOG (P5), order block (P6), change in state of delivery (S2), macros (T5), volume imbalance (P4), draw on liquidity, daily bias
- CTX: Daily-bias workflow: state HTF targets in advance (weekly/daily levels: mean threshold of an up-close candle, rejection block, buy-side liquidity, a daily volume imbalance and the prior candle's low); then intraday only waits for the opposing liquidity to be run and joins flow. Those HTF objectives persist all week and need not be re-derived daily (@10:15, @26:44, @27:47)
- RULE: In a bullish daily context the low-risk long is a buy AT or BELOW the 9:30 opening price; any move below the 9:30 open is treated as the manipulation leg (Judas) of a power-of-3 day, and the rest is one-sided trend-following (@13:51, @14:22, @17:55)
- RULE: Drill used: after price drops below the 9:30 open, sweeps relative equal lows and the new-day opening gap, then closes back above the NDOG and shifts delivery (CISD) -> buy; NWOG and an order block beneath act as support; wicks into the OB/NWOG are acceptable, do not demand a perfect tap (@25:11, @28:18)
- RULE: Holding discipline: do not trail the stop up under every minor swing; the plan is to let the position ride through the morning and accept that a pullback is scheduled at the lunch macro (@29:19)
- RULE: Lunch macro (11:30-13:30 NY) retracement locator: at 11:30 look back and take the first (nearest) swing low that formed AFTER 10:00; for a long-only morning, the macro tends to run price down to that low (stops of trailing longs) and often coincides with an NWOG/OB; after 13:30 the macro window is over and price is free to continue with the morning direction. Rally may also go through without any retracement (@29:51, @30:23, @32:55, @33:27)
- RULE: Last-hour logic: the last hour starts 15:00; take partial profit after 15:00 if nothing was taken; macro 15:15-15:45; the market-on-close program covers 15:45-16:00 (15 min, not 10) and drives price into HTF unfilled levels; an earnings-season print can add an algorithmic push at 16:01-16:15 (@14:52, @42:06, @42:38)
- MMXM: Daily candle template for the buy day: open near low, close near high; close settles slightly off the high of the day. Pulling a runner to the end of the day requires accepting the time needed for the daily candle to form and close (@14:52, @38:33)
- CTX: Bigger-than-normal event weeks (Jackson Hole Wed-Fri) are treated like an NFP week: trade Monday-Tuesday with the HTF plan, expect volatility, and expect the market to want to complete its objective early (@27:15, @43:42)
- CTX: Position sizing by alignment: largest size on trades with daily orderflow, small size on counter-trend shorts (@48:21)
- EX: 2024-08-19 ES/NQ 1m and 15m: opened below 9:30, ran sell-side beneath the lows, entry at the "institutional order-flow entry drill" after NDOG reclaim, rally above the NWOG, 11:30 lunch-macro dip to the post-10:00 low and NWOG, then 15:45 run to the daily volume imbalance; roughly 200+ handles on the day (@18:26, @30:54, @40:35)
- skip: Large share is commentary (rebuttals to critics, depth-of-market/orderflow criticism, latency, language, his son's pace); no codable content there.

### 013 | 20240820 | Gap-based model: NDOG/NWOG draws, ORG, quadrants/CE, partials on short-term highs
tags: NDOG/NWOG (P5), volume imbalance (P4), FVG (P1), consequent encroachment, opening range gap, EQH/EQL (L4), partials, price delivery continuum, institutional order flow entry drill
- DEF: Consequent encroachment (CE) = midpoint of a gap/FVG/volume imbalance; "mean threshold" is reserved for the midpoint of an order block. A wick-only connection between two candles counts as a gap (volume imbalance = body-to-body space where only a wick bridges) and is quartered and CE-tracked like a gap (@14:27, @14:57)
- DEF: NDOG date convention: gap between 17:00 settlement and 18:00 reopen is labelled with the NEXT trading day; keep it on the chart for ~5 days; NWOG for ~5 weeks (older ones still react but go stale) (@10:17, @06:42)
- RULE: Beginner model = gap-to-gap: when bullish, target an NDOG/NWOG above price (the "magnet") with relative equal highs sitting just below or at it; when bearish, target an NDOG/NWOG below with relative equal lows just above it (@05:42, @06:12)
- RULE: Gap partitioning for tracking fill: split an HTF volume imbalance into quadrants (low, lower-quadrant midpoint = CE, upper quadrant, high); evaluate (1) can price reach the low edge, (2) how fast, (3) behaviour on arrival: stops short vs slices through to CE (@11:50, @21:58)
- RULE: FVG usage in a continuation: best case price only trades into the upper half of a buy-side FVG and leaves the lower half open (signals strength); an overshoot is allowed provided candle BODIES stay above the FVG/wick CE and only wicks pierce; a lower-TF (15s) FVG body-respect inside a 1m FVG confirms (@20:53, @24:00)
- RULE: Opening range gap (ORG; prior 16:15 settle to 9:30 open): quarter it and keep it on the chart all day; after a gap-down open, a long toward the gap midpoint (CE) is very high probability and needs no directional bias; fills often stop at the CE then continue with the gap direction; a gap that is bullish-closed is held as support on retests (@38:19, @39:22, @49:42)
- RULE: Partial taking: when long, every break above a short-term high is a candidate to scale out; prefer a partial over trailing the stop; after a partial, move stop to breakeven/cover costs; milestone partials at pre-set minor targets (gap low, CE) (@32:10, @52:45, @55:18)
- RULE: Entry drill (1m/15s): drop below 9:30 open into NDOG low, sweep lows, displacement up, FVG forms -> buy order at the FVG candle high plus one tick; a later pullback into an OB is tolerable; failure to fill a lower quadrant is fine because it leaves the target for later (@51:13, @52:14)
- MMXM: Trade management logic: define the objective (CE/lower quadrant) in advance but success = having room to distribute; exits are valid even if the final draw is never hit; distribute into premium near the highs (@46:10, @60:56)
- CTX: Bias not needed for ORG-fill and gap trades; HTF bias (last Tuesday's daily volume-imbalance objective) gives which direction to favour; a day can end without reaching the draw (@38:50, @60:56)
- EX: 2024-08-20 NQ 1m/15s: gap-down open, ran up to NDOG (8/20) and ORG; reached HTF daily volume imbalance low (19,868.25) and its lower-quadrant CE; short-term-high breaks used for partials (@16:00, @26:00, @52:14)
- skip: Heavy commentary on partial-taking philosophy, graph-paper tape tracking anecdote, session-length housekeeping, spoofing/DOM remarks.

### 016 | 20240825 | Journaling template for the 2024-08-23 session: HTF levels, balanced price range, first-presented FVG, Event Horizon
tags: volume imbalance (P4), order block (P6), balanced price range (P3), propulsion block (P10), turtle soup (L7), NDOG/NWOG (P5), ORG, first presentation FVG, inversion FVG (P2), Event Horizon, equal highs/lows (L4), macros (T5)
- DEF: Volume imbalance = two adjacent candles whose BODIES do not overlap at all (even a 1-tick separation counts) irrespective of candle colour; wicks may overlap or cross it and do not negate it. CE of it = midpoint of the body-to-body space (@09:08, @10:10)
- RULE: Respect test for a bearish HTF volume imbalance: if price has gone through it then left lower, a valid bearish read never trades back into its upper (premium) half; the up-close candle that was broken becomes a bearish OB whose 50% (mean threshold) is the short zone (@11:14, @12:14)
- DEF: "Balanced price range" (this lecture's use) = a range that has been traded up-down-up-down delivering both sides, with propulsion blocks inside, which price then leaves with displacement. Buy-side stops above it are high-resistance liquidity: expect failure to reach it; if price does break it, treat as manual intervention (news) and stand aside (@15:17, @16:20, @18:25)
- RULE: First-presented FVG: the first FVG formed in the 09:30-10:00 range; the three-candle pattern may not start before the 09:31 candle (its imbalance candle must exist in both ETH and RTH data - gaps built on the 09:30 candle are ignored). Carry it all day as a reference; price is expected to react at it again (lunch and into 15:45 low) (@20:29, @38:30, @42:37)
- RULE: Opening-range reading: when 09:30 high/low form clean equal highs AND equal lows, wait for the first side to be taken - the first-taken side is the fake; do not want to see multi-candle accumulation in the CE of a counter FVG (a reversal that does not accumulate at CE signals another push, e.g. another drive above equal highs) (@21:33, @26:43)
- RULE: Short template (bearish day): sweep of buy side that fails to reach the NDOG above, then candle closes below the opening price (CISD); anywhere between that open and the OB mean threshold is a short, add (pyramid) on the way; stop above the OB; turtle soup at the equal highs validates the bearish OB (@24:41, @25:12)
- RULE: ORG: gap between 16:14/16:15 RTH close and 09:30 open; a gap-up open is a premium ORG, gap-down is a discount ORG; CE of the ORG is reached about 70% of the time per the lecturer; undelivered portion of the ORG is an intraday draw (delivered here during the lunch hour) (@34:20, @35:23)
- DEF: Event Horizon (new PD array, gray/dark pool): the midpoint zone between an NDOG/NWOG above and an NDOG/NWOG below price (any combination of the two types; both gaps must be within 60 days; quarter it - upper quadrant, CE, lower quadrant); price is drawn into it and bodies tend not to exceed its upper quadrant on a bearish day (@29:47, @31:49, @32:19)
- DEF: Nested NDOG inside an NWOG (the new day gap sits inside the new week gap) is a strong magnet where both coincide (@28:46)
- RULE: Final-hour macro 15:15-15:45: carry the first-presented FVG forward all day; the low of the day can form at its bottom around 15:45 (@42:06, @43:08)
- CTX: A Fed-chair speech at 10:00 produces manipulation - the first move is a fake; trade after news (@26:43, @41:35)
- CTX: Journal protocol: per day, log HTF levels in advance, ORG, NDOG/NWOG, first-presented FVG, highs/lows of day and their cause (@45:45)
- EX: 2024-08-23 NQ 1m (RTH): equal highs raided by a run to the daily SIBI high of 2024-07-23 and the 07-22 bearish OB mean threshold (19,881.75), turtle soup, drop through NDOG, inversion FVG, ran about 200+ handles down into NWOG (08/18)/NDOG nest, ORG CE fill at lunch, low at 15:45 on first-presented FVG (@23:07, @28:15, @35:53, @43:08)
- skip: First half is journaling advice and refs to prior streams.

### 017 | 20240826 | "Impeding Barriers": weak opening gap protocol, immediate rebalance, nested gaps; rest is trader psychology
tags: opening range gap, NDOG/NWOG (P5), immediate rebalance, FVG/inversion FVG (P2), EQL (L4), volume imbalance (P4), rejection block (P9), CISD (S2), mindset
- RULE: Weak-gap filter: if the 09:30 open is "unchanged" relative to the prior RTH settle (under about 30-40 handles on NQ; 20 handles = 20.00 points), do nothing until 10:00, then wait for the Silver Bullet window / a clearer setup; a large gap invites volatility and more protraction, a tiny one is unreliable and slows the day (@09:41, @12:16, @13:49, @16:58)
- DEF: Pre-open, the prior RTH settle (16:14/16:15 close) is labelled ORG high/low once the open prints; the ORG CE must be measured between settle and the 09:30 open, not from the nearest wick (@08:08)
- DEF: Immediate rebalance (new): a single candle with a range clearly larger than recent candles and closing near its extreme, whose NEXT candle retraces the entire body/range of that candle (back to its low for a down candle). In a bearish context this is a continuation signature (large range, speed ahead); it is not a reversal and is only valid if the rebalance occurs on the very next candle; works on all TFs, and is the opposite of a gap that stays open (@28:36, @32:09, @33:10)
- DEF: A three-candle pattern is not automatically an FVG for the model; the narrative matters. A grey/unqualified gap becomes an inversion FVG only if price displaced down from beyond it after it was broken (wick through does not qualify) (@21:06, @22:39)
- RULE: Inversion-FVG sell template (15m FVG traded through): after it is broken, expect a retest into the gap; short as long as price does not return above the gap's midpoint (wick to CE tolerated, bodies stay in lower half); then enter on the first FVG on a 15s/30s/45s/1m chart after displacement away (institutional order-flow entry drill) and target the next sell-side liquidity or inefficiency (@37:50, @50:18)
- RULE: Bodies-not-wicks reading: price that closes inside a PD array (e.g. a 15m FVG) means nothing has turned; a counter-trend long needs bodies to leave and hold; do not pick bottoms (@35:13, @38:50, @44:10)
- MMXM: Nested NDOG (08/23) inside NWOG (08/18) is the primary sell-side draw, with old low 19,591.75 below; wait for a move up into the 15m FVG/CIBI to halfway as the sell entry, otherwise expect speed down with large candles (@38:20, @39:53, @40:24)
- CTX: Heavy-resistance read on a daily/hourly range that delivered both sides: fast fill of it signals a weeks-long turn; slow fill = no change (@70:21, @71:22)
- CTX: Don't take indicator divergences (RSI/stoch) as reversal evidence; hidden/type-2 divergence is trend-following (@83:40)
- CTX: Models are mutually exclusive by market type (choppy vs trending vs reversal); do not switch model to find a trade that day; no trade = acceptable. Lecturer's models listed: OTE, 2022 model, Silver Bullet, NDOG/NWOG + first-presented FVG (@77:02, @86:44)
- RULE: Risk rules for the beginner model (non-ICT-specific): always a structural stop, one contract (micro) first, one winner or one loser ends the day, partials and limit orders to pre-handle first runner anxiety (@94:54, @117:34, @166:42)
- EX: 2024-08-26 NQ 1m/15m/50m: small ORG, opened near settle, 15m buy-side/sell-side inefficiency swept, immediate rebalance on a big down 1m candle, 15m FVG retest from below failed, sell-side targeted with NDOG/NWOG nest (@29:06, @38:50, @53:23)
