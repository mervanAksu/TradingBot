- RULE: Hybrid gap zone: when the Friday close -> Monday open gap overlaps a volume imbalance, measure the gap from the Friday candle BODY close (not wick low) to the Monday open; use its midpoint (CE), upper quadrant and lower quadrant as graded reaction/target levels (@02:06, @13:52)
- DEF: NWOG measured Friday close (not wick) to Monday/Sunday open; its midpoint is treated as the "low hanging fruit" objective, and as the retracement magnet within the week (@14:22, @31:12)
- RULE: Sell-side pool qualification: a low, an equal low and a slightly higher low clustered together is a valid sell-side liquidity pool, i.e. eligible as draw on liquidity (@03:41)
- RULE: TGIF (Thank God It's Friday): from Thursday ~13:00 NY to Friday close, price tends to retrace roughly 20-30% of the weekly range after an exhaustive weekly move. Check by fibbing the week's low-to-high on a weekly chart; held even with a doji/long-wick weekly candle (@05:13, @05:43, @14:52)
