- DEF: "Volume imbalance" (VI) = body-to-body separation: close of one candle vs open of the next, wicks overlapping; the wicks may still "do the damage" and trade through the zone. Zero-body (open=close) candle in the middle is ignored by the algorithm; reference is the neighbouring bodies (@31:28, @58:22, @59:54)
- DEF: Real liquidity void / "common gap" = wick-to-wick gap with no trading at all between candle A's low and candle B's high; distinct from a VI. A zone becomes "balanced" once later candles deliver both buy-side and sell-side through it (paint-roller idea), after which only wicks can violate it (@60:24, @61:28)
- DEF: Dual-VI daily PD array: when candle 2's body sits inside the span between two successive VIs, box from the first to second VI is encapsulated and graded with a Fib into low / lower quadrant / midpoint (EQ) / upper quadrant / high; PD arrays are expected to form around the quadrant levels (@31:58, @33:28, @45:58)
