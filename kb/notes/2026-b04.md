- Top mechanical rules:

  2. Wick-CE rule: a wick is graded by its 50%; bodies may not close beyond CE against the idea (above CE for bearish wick-reversal shorts, below for longs); no touch of CE at all = extra strength (073, 067, 071, 074).

  5. Candle-count limit inside an inefficiency: 1-2 candles fast; 3-5 candles reduce probability; by candle 5 must displace (066 @41:39-44:11).
