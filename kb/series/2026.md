## Core teachings
1. Time first, price second; draw first, pattern second. Name the liquidity or inefficiency price is reaching for; with no clear draw, no trade. Only two things deliver price: a run to buy/sell-side liquidity, or a run to an inefficiency (2026-b12/143@09:19, @40:00; 2026-b02/029@41:41; 2026-b04/060@02:03).
2. One-sidedness filter. Trade only when one reading is overwhelmingly favoured; if both sides can be argued or the stop cannot be framed cleanly, stand aside; skipping is not a loss (2026-b07/106@10:31, @20:38; 2026-b09/120@18:31; 2026-b04/073@43:17).
3. Daily/weekly premise first, then transpose down: a 1m idea without a daily/weekly draw is low probability (2026-b02/031@33:27; 2026-b04/060@02:03; 2026-b03/049@04:04). Weekend routine: previous month/week/session highs and lows, nearer pool on the HTF is the path of least resistance (2026-b02/034@04:25, @28:04).
4. Highs/lows form at (a) liquidity or (b) a key inefficiency; use that dichotomy to select the draw (2026-b01/008@26:52).
5. RTH opening range gap (ORG) = prior-day RTH final print (16:14/16:15 NY) to the 09:30 open, viewed on RTH data. Its CE is the first-30-min draw, claimed ~70% reached by 10:00 (a stat to test, not trust). Premium gap (open above settle) = look for shorts first; discount gap = longs (2026-b10/124@08:28; 2026-b11/133@10:18; 2026-b12/140@81:37; 2026-b07/107@32:58).
6. Gradient grid: any gap, wick, range, block or the 07:00-09:00 range is split into quadrants (25/50/75) and octants (12.5% steps); candle BODIES are judged against those lines, wicks may overshoot. "Levels, not zones" (2026-b03/044@12:17; 2026-b03/046@27:07; 2026-b10/124@12:00; 2026-b08/115@79:57).
7. Tethering: a FVG/OB/IFVG counts only if its key candle (middle one, or the volume-imbalance candle) touches a gradient level, a key range extreme or a higher-TF array (or it is the first presented FVG of the move). Untethered gaps are "common gaps" and ignored (2026-b03/046@16:20; 2026-b04/073@35:26; 2026-b10/131@47:53; 2026-b11/133@30:38).
8. Body-in-half test for every PD array: bearish array = bodies stay in the lower half (wicks may poke the upper half); bullish = bodies stay in the upper half. Not reaching CE = strength of the original side; a body closing through = array failed/flipped (2026-b02/025@09:09; 2026-b02/030@12:00; 2026-b09/116@07:02; 2026-b07/106@16:41).
9. Wick = gap: grade a prominent wick by CE (50%) and quadrants; a wick adjacent/left of a gap overrides the gap; a close beyond the wick CE signals continuation (2026-b01/001@17:58; 2026-b07/104@10:57; 2026-b08/115@06:08; 2026-b10/129@46:20).
10. Inversion FVG (IFVG) needs a candle body CLOSE through the gap (unlike an OB, where trading through the open suffices); wick-through does not invert; a gap that is closed back through can revert ("reclaimed") (2026-b05/082@03:34; 2026-b09/116@55:36; 2026-b02/027@08:43).
11. First presented FVG (FPFVG): first FVG formed on displacement after a window start (07:00 pre-market, 09:30 open, 10:00 silver-bullet hour, or right after a turtle soup); a tiny gap without displacement is common; on NQ a gap under ~10 handles is probably not it (2026-b12/140@52:03, @67:21; 2026-b12/143@13:26; 2026-b10/131@52:29; 2026-b01/013@00:33).
12. Event horizon = the 0.5 line between two same-side pools (two lows, two highs, or a swept low and the next low); partial point, add point and strength gauge: a close beyond it raises odds the next pool is taken (2026-b05/075@06:46; 2026-b09/119@27:24; 2026-b04/064@11:40; 2026-b01/004@07:50).
13. 07:00-09:00 NY is its own dealing range: a trending 7-9 is followed by a choppy AM session; a consolidating 7-9 by a trending AM (07:00-08:30 on 08:30-news days) (2026-b02/029@01:35; 2026-b02/030@04:13; 2026-b01/004@03:08).
14. Macro windows are the last 10 min of an hour plus the first 10 of the next (xx:50-xx+1:10); 09:50-10:10, 10:50-11:10, 13:50-14:10 and 15:15-15:45 recur; xx:17/20/40 are not macros (2026-b01/008@35:05; 2026-b10/125@13:28; 2026-b05/087@07:31).
15. Lunch macro: window 11:30-13:30; setup forms in the first hour; the draw is the first/most prominent low (or high) formed after 10:00; ends 13:30, then 13:30-14:00 is the PM opening range (2026-b10/128@69:45; 2026-b05/088@05:44; 2026-b01/012@31:18).
16. News: do not hold through 08:30 prints (CPI, PPI, NFP) or FOMC; do not trade the 08:30 candle; delivery is two-stage (spike, then reversal into/through the origin); fade after ~15-20 min (2026-b01/004@04:39, @38:14; 2026-b02/036@04:03; 2026-b04/065@13:21).
17. TGIF: after a one-directional week the Thursday ~13:00-Friday retracement is 20-30% (to 35-40%) of the weekly range; a consolidation week retraces 40%+; if missed, the following Monday does it (2026-b01/015@00:32; 2026-b06/098@12:16; 2026-b07/105@23:04; 2026-b10/128@38:11).
18. Regime texture: HRLR = overlapping candle ranges ("barcode"), gaps re-used for many candles; LRLR = porous, stretched candles that leave gaps. Do not trade HRLR; the same array used by 1-2 candles is healthy, 3-5 candles lowers odds (2026-b10/128@10:40; 2026-b04/066@41:39; 2026-b09/116@73:16; 2026-b05/084@33:33).
19. Swing points and anchors are body-based: wicks "do the damage", bodies carry the narrative; fib anchors at the body edge furthest reached (2026-b03/049@04:34; 2026-b08/114@90:06; 2026-b09/121@36:20).
20. Management is partials-first and exit-before-the-level: fund the trade with the first partial, stop to cost, exit a few ticks before the pool (posted/round levels get raided), never wait for the terminus (2026-b02/032@14:53, @18:50, @58:59; 2026-b06/095@44:32; 2026-b12/142@39:19).
21. Time distortion: if the 1m is unreadable chop, step up a TF (5m, 15m, 1h) until the array actually in use appears (2026-b08/113@101:13; 2026-b09/118@53:33).
22. Seasonality/calendar gates: Jan-Mar choppy (engage from first Tuesday of Feb), spring and Sep-Nov cleaner, summer/holiday weeks HRLR, US-holiday-on-weekend Monday = no-touch, NFP-week beginners stop after Wednesday morning (2026-b12/140@71:59; 2026-b10/128@20:54; 2026-b04/070@04:39; 2026-b01/019@28:17).
23. Contract handling: draw HTF levels from the continuous (back-adjusted toggle) series, execute on the front month, mark both; roll week ~Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec Tuesday, reduce size (2026-b09/118@40:09; 2026-b09/120@01:45; 2026-b06/098@04:35; 2026-b07/107@19:40).
24. Opposing-crowd filter: levels publicised by the instructor or round projections attract raids; do not rest limits on them (2026-b02/032@31:46; 2026-b06/097@23:51; 2026-b03/056@25:14).
25. "Market maker" = the delivery algorithm working off time + OHLC bodies, not a dealer; no footprint or L2 needed (2026-b06/100@21:52; 2026-b04/070@02:34; 2026-b10/124@30:50).

## Definitions vs DRAFT / other series
- L1/L3 sessions: Asia session 19:00-05:00 with Asia killzone only 19:00-21:00; London killzone 02:00-05:00 (core 02-04), London session to ~10:00-11:00; NY frame 09:30-16:00. Another file uses the 00:00-07:00 overnight range for the 07:00 run. CLAUDE.md Asia 20:00-00:00 differs; make the overnight range a parameter (2026-b12/140@24:33, @31:21; 2026-b12/143@18:03; 2026-b11/139@15:53).
- L4 EQH/EQL: ordered geometry, no tolerance band: anchor on the older point, later point falls slightly short; "smooth" singles allowed (2026-b12/140@66:19).
- L5/L6: HRLR/LRLR defined by candle overlap and gap structure, not by pool-to-pool arrays (2026-b10/128@10:40; 2026-b07/103@70:24).
- P1 FVG: displacement-formed, tethered, with a ~10-handle NQ floor; the wick left of a gap overrides it; candle-1 stop (2026-b12/140@52:03; 2026-b11/139@19:02).
- P2 IFVG: body close through plus first-utilisation history; reclaimed state; "AAA" strength tier (2026-b05/085@33:47; 2026-b06/099@28:25).
- P4 VI: body-to-body, >= 1 tick difference; stays alive until a body covers it (2026-b04/073@34:19; 2026-b10/128@17:47).
- P5: NDOG/NWOG on 16:59/18:00 still used, but the daily workhorse is the RTH ORG on 16:14/16:15 (2026-b01/019@32:23; 2026-b10/124@08:28). Not in DRAFT.
- P6 OB: open of the (largest) candle is the CISD; mean threshold 50%; no close needed if liquidity swept (2026-b04/067@00:01; 2026-b04/073@18:24).
- P7 breaker: lowest-bodied down-close candle of the high-low-higher-high sequence (2026-b03/053@11:16).
- P10 propulsion block: needs parent OB on a gradient level (2026-b09/122@30:18).
- S2 CISD: trade through the open of the delivering series, a close is not required (DRAFT demands a first close) (2026-b03/051@37:09).
- S1 MSS: engulfing alone is not a shift; displacement through a swing is (2026-b11/139@18:31). MSS is rarely named in this series.
- S3 premium/discount: applied to gaps, wicks, 07-09 range, FHDR, 3-month monthly range (50%), prior-day range (bearish market should not retrace past its 50%) (2026-b01/019@04:54; 2026-b05/075@65:45).
- S4 OTE: not used as 62-79%; the "3/4 of the range" and octant/quadrant ladder replace it (2026-b11/138@38:52).
- S7: PD-array lookback = last 20 trading days (then 40/60); 3-day lookback for intraday liquidity (2026-b12/140@19:57; 2026-b03/044@52:12).
- T1: opening range = 09:30-10:00 (30 min); first-hour dealing range 09:30-10:30; PM opening range 13:30-14:00; lunch 11:30-13:30 (CLAUDE.md says 12:00-13:30) (2026-b08/115@84:00; 2026-b09/123@01:34).
- T5: macro = xx:50-xx+1:10; 07:40-08:10, 08:50-09:10, 09:50-10:10, 10:50-11:10, 11:50-12:10, 13:50-14:10, 14:50-15:10, 15:15-15:45 appear; 05:50 once; "macro" also used for big-picture time frames (2026-b08/115@41:36; 2026-b05/082@01:01).

## MMXM material
### 1. How the series names the model
ICT's 2026 walk-throughs use one fixed vocabulary, always applied after the fact on an annotated chart; none gives numeric stage boundaries (2026-b04/067@03:05; 2026-b09/123@18:33; 2026-b08/115@08:42; 2026-b11/139 summary). The vocabulary is: original consolidation (OC); a run away from it; return to/retest of the OC; accumulation (or distribution) stage 1; reaccumulation (or redistribution) stage 2; smart money reversal (SMR); low-risk entry; first-stage distribution (sell) or accumulation (buy); second-stage redistribution/reaccumulation; terminus. "Buy side of the curve" = everything left of the extreme; "sell side of the curve" = from the extreme down; the objective is to be on the sell side of the curve from a key high (2026-b04/072@17:34). A sell side of a curve delivers a buy model (2026-b07/103@113:57; 2026-b10/127@54:21).

### 2. Sell model (MMSM) - order of events
Best-described pair: 2026-b04/067 (2026-07-08, NQ 1m, ETH+RTH tape) and 2026-b04/072 (2026-07-02, NQ 1m, jobs-data morning).
1. Original consolidation (OC) forms; its low is the eventual objective side.
2. Price leaves it and runs the other way (up for a sell model) = buy side of the curve; this leg is called first-stage accumulation/reaccumulation, with an optional second stage of reaccumulation (higher lows/steps in the retracement leg) (2026-b04/067@03:05, @05:10; 2026-b05/082@05:35).
3. SMR at the top: buy side taken (macro open), then a candle closes below an FVG/imbalance (validating it as inverted) after a breaker (high, low, higher high) forms (2026-b05/082@03:03).
4. Low-risk sell = retest of the first broken area (CE of the inversion gap / sell-side-imbalance gap), not the SMR bar itself (2026-b04/067@19:09; 2026-b04/072@13:56; 2026-b05/082@03:34).
5. First-stage distribution = first leg down after SMR.
6. Second-stage redistribution = retracement up into a premium array (breaker/IFVG/OB) followed by the largest and easiest leg down; trigger on 2026-07-08 = unicorn (bearish breaker overlapping a gap) inside the 10:50-11:10 macro with a wick up into a daily bearish OB that failed to close above; entry inside the breaker, stop above its high (2026-b04/067@12:55, @14:06). On 2026-07-02 the redistribution entry was the bearish OB (last up-close candle, already used twice) sold in its lower half, stop above the VI/OB high, adds on later returns (2026-b04/072@04:02-@07:08).
7. Target: sell-side below, normally through the OC low; exit may stop earlier at nearer relative equal lows or an event horizon when the Asia/London move already swept the OC zone (2026-b04/067@08:46, @19:39; 2026-b04/067@23:15 for the event-horizon partial).
Entry freedom: ICT states the trade may be entered at any of SMR, low-risk sell, first-stage distribution or second-stage redistribution (2026-b04/072@17:34). The 9:30 Judas into premium (jobs data on 07-02, NDOG CE on 07-08 with no body at/above CE) is read as the SMR leg, not bullish continuation (2026-b04/067@09:18; 2026-b04/072@13:56).
Same list, other wording: 2026-b08/115@08:42 (GBPUSD weekly/daily, 2026-03-28) lists OC, return to consolidation, accumulation 1, accumulation 2, reaccumulation, SMR, low-risk sell, distribution 1, redistribution 2 (largest move); valid while price stays below the IFVG that capped the prior week's high. 2026-b09/123@18:33 gives OC, accumulation/reaccumulation, SMR, low-risk sell, first and second distribution, with a rare extra leg "to take out the OC".
Worked 2026-06-02 NQ 1m (2026-b05/082): London relative equal lows = OC; rally taking buy side = first-stage accumulation; stage 2 absent in a fast move; SMR at the 05:50 macro open (buy side taken, close below FVG after breaker); low-risk sell = retrace into the small inversion gap CE (2 contracts, add on retrace, partial below the minor sell side, re-add on pullback); redistribution = largest leg that clears the OC equal lows and the preset terminus; time map 05:00-06:00 dead, 06:00 earliest trustworthy (2026-b05/082@01:01-@06:06). Result: short near the 30,5xx zone, exit at relative equal lows, then price reversed and made a new ATH (2026-b05/082@06:38, @07:08).

### 5. Fractal, completion and exceptions

## Session-phase logic
- Four-phase day (taught in 2016 lecture 1, restated): London trends, 07:00-09:00 consolidates, expansion, NY reversal; the next session after a trend is highly likely to consolidate or reverse; trend across London, 7-9 and RTH is rare (2026-b01/013@23:05; 2026-b02/025@24:32; 2026-b02/029@13:02).
- 7-9 classification: quick poke sweeping one side then returning to the midpoint = consolidation; protracted directional = trend (2026-b02/028@06:54; 2026-b02/029@01:35). Needs a quantified proxy.
- Trend -> choppy AM, consolidation -> expansion at 09:30; Judas = first protraction after 09:30 toward the pool, then reversal (2026-b02/031@16:26; 2026-b10/131@23:23).
- Consolidation day: wait for the trap (equal highs run inside 10:50-11:10), sell back through the 09:30 open (2026-b02/030@09:55).
- After a retracement-style morning: lunch macro retraces to the post-10:00 extreme, ends 13:30, PM opens 13:30-14:00, final-hour macros 14:50-15:10 and 15:15-15:45 (2026-b10/128@79:27; 2026-b10/125@13:28; 2026-b05/087@13:16).
- Weekday logic: Monday without news = poor, NFP Monday cleanest; CPI/PPI Wed/Thu; Thursday forms an intermediate high; Friday TGIF retrace; Friday must hit the weekly draw before opposite equal highs (2026-b04/066@31:49; 2026-b06/093@09:51; 2026-b03/058@31:00).
- Retracement vs reversal: premium ORG with a failed run (Judas) = reversal; a gap that never trades = gap-and-go; breaking outside the gap low and holding after 3/4 closure = abandon the closure and look deeper (2026-b11/138@69:33; 2026-b08/115@38:35).
- Skip days: a big impulsive push at ~07:00 predicts a lethargic morning; day before FOMC+CPI; US holiday on weekend Monday; Fed-speaker 10:00; rollover week; first weeks of January (2026-b06/093@41:52; 2026-b09/120@07:53; 2026-b04/071@01:03; 2026-b12/143@19:19).
