# Series digest: foundations
Sources: foundations-b01 (files 003-019), foundations-b02 (file 002). Refs `<batch>/<NNN>@mm:ss`.
Coverage warning: the notes contain NO file that teaches the full Market Maker Buy/Sell Model (no phases, SMR, 1st/2nd distribution). The "Market Maker Primer" files present (Primer 003/006/008/010/023/024 = b01/006-011) teach structure, PO3, weekly profile and OTE only. See the MMXM section.

## Core teachings
- PO3/AMD is fractal on any bar with an open: accumulate around the open, manipulate against the true direction (sweep stops beyond the consolidation edge), distribute to the far extreme. Bullish: buy at/below open; bearish: sell at/above (b01/009@01:17, @07:03, @12:44).
- Weekly open = Sunday opening print, used as the weekly premium/discount pivot (b01/008@02:20, @09:07).
- Bullish week: weekly low forms between Sunday open and Wed 07:00 NY, best odds Tue London to Wed NY open; buy below the weekly open. Bearish mirrors (b01/008@10:38, @26:07).
- Invalidation: a new weekly low after Wed NY open voids a bullish week; after Wednesday price should not return to the weekly open (b01/008@13:13, @19:30).
- Monday is mostly ignored as the weekly extreme; sell limits just above Monday's high/equal highs before Tue London (b01/008@18:26, @37:24).
- Weekly bias alternative: 50% of the prior Friday's daily candle; down-close Friday -> bearish while price stays below it (b02/002@01:35, @03:41).
- NWOG measured from Friday body close to Monday open; CE and quadrants are graded targets/magnets (b02/002@02:06, @14:22).
- TGIF: Thu ~13:00 NY to Fri close tends to retrace 20-30% of the weekly range (b02/002@05:43).
- Liquidity: equal highs/lows or single obvious extremes are pools; run -> rejection -> next opposing pool, ping-pong (b01/010@01:43, @20:45; b01/015@03:10).
- Sell-side pool can be a low + equal low + slightly higher low cluster (b02/002@03:41).
- Key-level set for daily work: last week H/L, previous month H/L, last 3 months H/L, rolling 12 months H/L (b01/010@00:41).
- Do not trade the middle (~50%) of a range; wait for a key level (b01/010@19:44).
- Top-down order: seasonality -> quarterly shift -> driver -> profile (consolidating/trending/retracing) -> correlated markets -> SMT -> PD arrays -> key levels -> bias (b01/005@06:47, @35:30).
- Profile logic: consolidating -> expect expansion, ask which side's liquidity goes first; trending -> continue, don't pick tops; retracing -> find the array that ends it (b01/005@29:21).
- Structure break = HTF level reached, then most recent short-term swing taken on the HTF; afterwards trade only retracements (b01/006@11:30, @15:37).
- OTE = 62-79% retrace anchored on bodies, not wicks (b01/011@10:45, @13:48; b01/008@15:19).
- Measured-move/SD targets: repeat the leg (1x), plus 1.5/2.5/3/3.5x on trend days (b01/006@24:23; b01/018@06:10).
- NY continuation model: daily bias + London extreme -> 07:00-09:00 retrace sweeping equal lows -> OTE/FVG entry (b01/007@09:55, @13:29).
- Scaling is fixed-distance: FX +15/25/40 pips; ES +30/50/75 handles; day trade banks 75-80% at T1, swing ~50%; stop to BE; no aggressive trailing (b01/012@04:19; b01/013@02:14; b01/011@26:19).
- Hygiene: skip 08:30 high-impact prints (premark levels); no resting limits in 12:00-13:00 lunch; 1-2 trades/day, 3+ is a warning (b01/015@01:39; b01/014@09:18; b01/017@12:12).
- SMT (NQ/ES/YM analogue): one market holds a higher low while another prints a lower low = accumulation footprint; entry at a discount array (b01/003@28:56; b01/004@10:53, @17:40).

## Definitions vs DRAFT / other series
- L8 weekly open = Sunday first trade (price, no gap logic); NWOG here uses Friday BODY close to Monday open with CE/quadrants, not Fri close -> Sun open wick-agnostic (b01/008@02:20; b02/002@02:06). Midnight open (00:00 NY) is a daily premium/discount reference (b01/018@01:06).
- L1/L2/S7 horizon levels: previous week, previous ~30 days, last 3 months, rolling 12 months (b01/010@00:41).
- T1 windows differ from CLAUDE.md (07:00-10:00 NY): NY KZ 07:00-09:00; NY session 07:00-14:00; study window 08:30-11:00; London KZ 02:00-05:00; London close ~10:00-12:00 (b01/007@07:23; b01/012@03:14; b01/018@04:39).
- S4 OTE is body-anchored (b01/011@13:48), sweet spot not stated beyond 62-79%.
- P6 bearish OB = last up-close candle, reaction at body/open; its open = CISD line (b01/014@03:39; b02/002@15:23). Consistent with S2.
- P1 naming: bearish FVG = SIBI; CE = midpoint (b01/018@01:37).
- Swing: short-term = 3-bar fractal; intermediate/long by nesting (b01/006@28:31). Matches DRAFT S6.
- L4: pool may be equal lows plus a slightly higher low (b02/002@03:41); large-fund liquidity sits at equal/obvious extremes (b01/010@24:51).
- Overbought/oversold = side of weekly open relative to HTF bias, not an oscillator (b01/008@04:59).
- ES "SD" = OTE swing range projected 0.5x/1.0x (b01/013@02:14); FX version 1/1.5/2.5/3/3.5x (b01/018@06:10). Not CBDR-based (T3 not confirmed here).
- Mega-trade/SMT-stock material (b01/003-005) is a different instrument family; "mega trade" for bonds = >= ~10k USD/contract (b01/004@42:30).

## MMXM material (Market Maker Primer and related)
Present in the notes: nothing that states the Market Maker Model with phases. What exists is adjacent scaffolding:
- Fractal PO3 per session: open -> counter move makes session low -> expansion to session high -> pullback to close (b01/007@07:54); weekly PO3 with Judas swing against bias, then expansion through Friday (b01/008@09:37).
- Sequence usable as the manipulation->distribution part: HTF key level reached -> sweep of resting stops beyond equal highs/old high -> rejection back inside -> break of the last short-term swing (MSS) -> retrace entry (b01/006@11:30; b01/010@20:45; b01/018@02:07).
- Distribution targets: opposite resting stops ~20 pips beyond old extreme (b01/009@10:42, @14:48); ladder of pools/gaps nearest first (b02/002@30:11); intraday PO3 read at a premarked premium array, expansion down, cover at daily FVG (b01/015@09:21).
- Templates: bullish monthly = seasonal buy month + retrace into discount OB + consolidation under buy stops -> target range high (b01/005@51:29); daily reversal day after sell-side below prior week/quarter low (b01/014@02:08).
- Failure example: raid above an old high that failed as turtle soup (b01/004@32:45).
- Partials at each leg and stop to BE are consistent with the user decision (b01/008@32:46; b01/011@26:19).
Not found in these notes: original consolidation, buy/sell-side curve, SMR labeling, 1st/2nd distribution legs, re-accumulation/re-distribution levels. The spec's phases 2-3 and 6 cannot be verified from this series. The relevant MMXM Primer lectures may sit in another series or were not in these batches.

## Session-phase logic
- Ask consolidating? -> trending? -> retracing? first (b01/005@29:21; b01/006@37:42); consolidation precedes expansion; late December is typically consolidation (b01/010@07:57).
- Trend day: London forms one extreme, 10:00-12:00 NY the other (b01/018@05:09). NY is continuation of London or a full reversal; London agreeing with daily bias = loaded (b01/007@09:25).
- PM structure on ES: 13:00 push, 14:00 drop to level, ~15:45 market-on-close algorithm window (b01/014@12:54); 10:00 hour rally then lunch retrace and attack of lows into ~13:15 (b01/015@07:49).
- Choppy range: trade inside, size down, partial only (b01/019@21:17; b02/002@08:13).
- Seasonality: clean volatility mid-Apr to Jun/Jul, late Aug, Sep-Nov, early Dec (b01/019@12:35).
