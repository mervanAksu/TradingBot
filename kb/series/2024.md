## Core teachings
1. Time first, price second: every model needs a time of day, a target (a liquidity pool or an inefficiency) and a reason; without the clock no pattern is used (2024-b01/001@13:58; 2024-b12/038@73:46; 2024-b02/005@56:51).
2. Price is delivered only to two kinds of places: resting stops beyond old highs/lows, or inefficiencies (gaps / imbalances). The largest orders sit beyond swing extremes, never inside an imbalance (2024-b09/026@30:06; 2024-b13/042@53:12; 2024-b08/024@90:14).
3. The beginner morning model is one trade per day, one contract, one loss ends the day, a clean win may also end it (2024-b03/006@154:04; 2024-b05/011@158:12; 2024-b06/017@94:54). Doing nothing is a valid position when both sides can be argued (2024-b01/001@113:24; 2024-b07/020@52:33; 2024-b10/033@61:38).
4. "Smooth vs jagged" (b01): a clean, flat, relative-equal pool that formed after the window start is the magnet; an area already traded through many times is spent (2024-b01/001@69:59, @121:59).
5. The 07:00 / 08:00 / 09:00 window frame (b01-b03): after each hour start, a ~30 min counter-move (Judas) against the pool's side is expected, then the run to the untouched pool (2024-b01/002@180:00; 2024-b02/003@15:39; 2024-b05/011@147:34).
6. Opening gaps are the priority draws: NDOG / NWOG first, then relative equal highs/lows and session extremes, then plain FVGs (2024-b02/003@114:00). A cluster of several gaps on one side of price picks the side (2024-b02/004@51:21; 2024-b03/006@95:32).
7. From mid-August the RTH opening range gap (ORG, prior RTH settle to 09:30 first print) takes over as the morning anchor: price tends to trade back to the gap midpoint inside 09:30-10:00 about 70% of the time (a claim; sample not given) (2024-b07/018@30:43; 2024-b08/022@15:20; 2024-b10/029@70:02; 2024-b12/040@04:29).
8. The "first presented FVG" (first 1m FVG whose three candles start at 09:31 or later) is the day's reference array and also the entry array of the beginner model (2024-b06/016@20:29; 2024-b07/019@60:59; 2024-b10/029@55:39; 2024-b11/036@38:52).
9. Candle bodies decide respect; wicks may overshoot (up to about 1-2 ticks for bodies on NQ, a few ticks for wicks, "mohawk"). Closing through the far side = failed (2024-b04/008@19:02; 2024-b07/018@81:35; 2024-b05/011@63:11).
10. Midpoint vocabulary: CE = midpoint of a gap, FVG, volume imbalance or wick; mean threshold (MT) = midpoint of an order-block body; equilibrium = midpoint of a dealing range (2024-b03/006@72:42; 2024-b06/013@14:27; 2024-b10/029@109:18; 2024-b14/048@74:50).
11. A wick is a gap: its CE is a level, bodies failing to close beyond it show weakness (2024-b04/008@07:46, @30:46; 2024-b05/011@175:35; 2024-b09/026@44:00).
12. A PD array is half-and-half: when it works, price only uses the half nearest the entry and leaves the far half unvisited (shorts: no bodies in the upper half; longs: none in the lower half). Touching CE = weak; closing through = failed and the array flips to inverse (2024-b02/003@33:46; 2024-b07/018@83:07; 2024-b09/028@43:17, @55:15; 2024-b11/036@104:35).
13. A newly formed array must prove itself: only a fast, displacing departure after the tap counts; "a PD array alone is not a signal" (2024-b08/021@18:52; 2024-b10/033@07:46).
14. Draw first, entry second: a trade needs a stated target (liquidity or inefficiency) and at least ~20 NQ handles (10 handles minimum on the loosest wording, ES ~5) between entry and target (2024-b03/007@190:15; 2024-b05/010@75:10; 2024-b09/028@98:04; 2024-b10/031@28:56).
15. Management is partials-first: first partial at the nearest relevant extreme or at ~50% of the run, then stop to cost, never widen, trail only after the position has "rolled away" (2024-b03/006@18:18, @102:40; 2024-b06/013@52:45; 2024-b07/019@19:03; 2024-b07/020@102:39).
16. Day-type matters more than shape: news days, Mondays, Fridays/TGIF, a day after a large-range day, holiday weeks and decoupled indices each change whether to trade at all (see Session-phase logic).
17. Market regime = delivery texture: LRLR = separated large candles, gaps left open, targets hit without re-tests; HRLR = overlapping candles, arrays re-touched and closed through, imprecise reactions. Trade aggressively only in LRLR (2024-b11/036@93:23; 2024-b13/043@51:04; 2024-b14/049@113:55).
18. Opposing-view filter: when the retail crowd (chat, streamers) agrees with a weak idea, skip it; not codable (2024-b01/002@258:51; 2024-b05/011).
19. Contract month and rolls matter for carried levels: NQ quarterly, roll ~2 weeks before expiry, carry old levels across with the contract spread (2024-b07/019@28:18; 2024-b10/031@04:55; 2024-b10/029@42:45).
20. Standard-deviation / fib projections are used only for range measurement and only together with a real level: 1, 2, 2.5 SD of a window range; -0.25/-0.5/-1 of a swing; 25% of the prior range beyond a broken high (2024-b01/002@217:40; 2024-b10/031@54:08; 2024-b14/051@01:07).

## Session-phase logic
- Early morning (07:00-09:30 NY, student window): consolidation / hunt for post-07:00 pools; first 30 min after each hour start = Judas counter-move; then run to the untouched pool (2024-b01/001@125:33; 2024-b02/003@15:39). 08:30 spools every day regardless of news; news only scales it (2024-b02/004@09:44; 2024-b13/044@29:53).
- 09:30-10:00 (opening range, 30 min): gap-driven phase: price travels to ORG CE (~70% claimed), first presented FVG forms, Judas leg first (a straight run from the open with no PD array in it is the fake; fade it) (2024-b09/028@61:05, @69:45; 2024-b06/012@13:51). Beginners do not enter in it (2024-b05/011@19:41) but later lectures do trade the first FVG after 09:31.

- Expansion with small re-accumulations to ~11:30; 10:30-11:30 typically forms the AM higher low (2024-b14/048@94:54).
- 11:30-13:30 lunch macro: counter-move into the nearest post-10:00 swing against the morning side; may also be a reversal for the day/week; finish AM positions by ~13:30 (2024-b05/010@31:08, @111:32; 2024-b05/011@150:07; 2024-b08/024@104:08).
