## Core teachings
1. Everything is a gap: FVG, volume imbalance, suspension block, opening range gap, NWOG/NDOG, and also every daily/weekly WICK. Each is graded with five levels (low, 25%, CE=50%, 75%, high); bodies are judged against the quadrants, wicks may overshoot (2025-b01/001@05:08; 2025-b02/006@39:16; 2025-b04/041@01:07; 2025-b04/033@02:34).
2. Body rule (the single most repeated test): in a bullish read bodies live in the upper half of the array and never close below its CE (gap/wick) or mean threshold (OB); bearish mirrored; wicks "colour outside the lines" (1-2 ticks on NQ up to the lower quadrant) without invalidating (2025-b01/002@116:36, @118:39; 2025-b03/026@26:24; 2025-b05/068@02:35; 2025-b06/072@04:58).
3. Failing to reach an array's CE (or far quadrant) is strength in the prevailing direction; reaching it fully is weakness. Never buy a full closure of a bullish FVG (2025-b02/006@17:45, @29:30; 2025-b05/055@07:17; 2025-b03/016@12:33).
4. Draw first: the day is planned from named liquidity (pre-market 07:00-09:30 / London / Asia / prior RTH highs and lows, NDOG/NWOG/ORG edges, daily wick CEs). Walk left from 09:30 and take the FIRST clean relative equal highs/lows met; stack several references on one side to pick the side (2025-b04/040@09:10; 2025-b04/034@18:12; 2025-b03/010@12:41).
5. Opening range = exactly 30 minutes (09:30-10:00 equities; 13:30-14:00 for the PM; 01:30-02:00 London; 07:00-07:30 NY-open killzone block); never 5 or 15 (2025-b01/003@09:42; 2025-b05/054@42:04; 2025-b06/082@18:33).
6. First presented FVG (FPFVG): the first displacement-sized 1m FVG formed after the session opens (first candle of the gap is the 09:31 bar or later; a 09:30-bar gap is excluded unless the ORG exception applies). It must be tied to real displacement that breaks structure after liquidity was taken, not a tick-sized crack, and not formed inside a balanced price range. It is carried forward for days/the week (2025-b01/001@25:48; 2025-b01/003@12:46; 2025-b04/049@08:14; 2025-b05/063@14:26; 2025-b06/077@24:44).
7. RTH opening range gap (ORG) is a different object from NDOG/NWOG: prior RTH settle (16:14 print; RTH ends 16:15) to the 09:30 open, on an RTH chart. Price is said to return to its CE about 70% of the time, ideally by ~10:00 (a claim, no sample); the odds depend on gap size (2025-b03/010@04:58; 2025-b01/001@22:46; 2025-b06/078@03:09).
8. Wick selection among a stack: grade the wick price actually respects / the lowest-reaching (premium wick below price) / the longer one; a premium wick that price trades above flips into a discount array and vice versa (2025-b02/006@11:04; 2025-b02/007@11:18; 2025-b03/015@05:10; 2025-b04/041@01:07).
9. Quadrant filter (b02): an FVG/OB is "high probability" only when one of its edges (or CE) coincides with a quadrant/CE of a graded wick, gap, ORG or the 20-day range; a quadrant through the middle of the FVG makes it an ordinary gap to skip. "Where" = the array on a quadrant, "when" = a macro window (2025-b02/004@126:02; 2025-b02/006@96:16, @119:24).
10. Macros are 20-minute windows xx:50 to xx+1:10 (journal them on 1m/5m), with the named set in use: 06:50-07:10, 07:50-08:10, 09:50-10:10, 10:50-11:10, 13:50-14:10, 14:50-15:10, 15:15-15:45, plus a close run from ~15:50; London 02:50-03:10, 03:30, 03:50-04:10. He rejects xx:20/xx:40 "macros" (2025-b05/054@74:56; 2025-b04/044@12:13; 2025-b04/050@01:41; 2025-b06/080@34:14; 2025-b06/074@00:09).
11. Inversion FVG (he writes "inversion", not "inverse"): a gap closed through by a body flips; entry is the return into it with bodies staying in the half nearest the new direction (bearish: lower half only; bullish: upper half only). A "reclaimed" FVG is a gap that price closed through and then re-took, so it resumes its original role (2025-b03/012@08:22; 2025-b03/022@09:25; 2025-b05/068@02:35; 2025-b06/075@02:52; 2025-b05/065@04:11).
12. Delivery regime = FVG cadence: expect roughly one FVG per 15-minute block; none = high-resistance liquidity run, stand aside or cut size and take partials early. Whip-saw/overlap with no open inefficiency = untradable "manual intervention" day (2025-b05/065@19:29; 2025-b05/064@01:08; 2025-b04/046@07:54).
13. Time overrides pattern: lunch macro 11:30-13:30 pulls against the AM leg; PM opening range 13:30-14:00; final-hour macros; the last hour is scalper-only (2025-b05/057@07:20; 2025-b02/004@141:42; 2025-b05/054@58:57).
14. Not picking tops: at all-time highs stay with continuation; shorts only intraday, short hold, to low-hanging pool; prior-close overshoot / immediate rebalance are the expected shapes (2025-b03/024@05:11, @09:50; 2025-b03/018@01:39; 2025-b03/012@21:09).
15. Venom (1m reversal model for the first hour), Gauntlet, Turtle Soup with deferred entry, IOFED, immediate rebalance: all described as entry refinements inside a pre-set bias/draw, never stand-alone (see table).
16. Gap size regimes (ORG, NQ handles): < ~20 ignore; 20-75 expect CE; 75-120 no edge; >= ~120 do not expect fill, only the quadrant at the open; several hundred = do not fade at the open (2025-b06/078@03:09; 2025-b06/082@24:11; 2025-b06/077@07:46).
17. Intermarket qualifiers only (never triggers): DXY lower = risk-on for NQ; SMT weighting S&P > Nasdaq >> Dow; decoupled DXY/gold/NQ falling together = high-resistance, stand aside (2025-b02/004@04:38; 2025-b01/002@173:59; 2025-b03/011@18:31).
18. Management style: scale in at FVG low edge / CE / retest, partials at successive prior lows or pools, stop to cost after the first partial, limit exits a tick or two inside the level, hard stops only, size down on news/gap days (2025-b06/075@00:46; 2025-b05/060@38:58; 2025-b04/052@19:12; 2025-b04/048@12:52).
19. Premise vs narrative vs bias: premise = liquidity logic that is unconditional ("must go down to go up"); narrative = path after the premise; bias = stance only after the trigger. Do not encode bias before the trigger (2025-b01/002@80:00).
20. Look-back: NWOG/NDOG/ORG/wick arrays stay relevant ~20/40/60 days (ORG quadrants carried four sets: today + previous three days); a filled gap's levels stay usable (2025-b04/043@15:37; 2025-b02/007@31:59; 2025-b03/010@34:37).

## Definitions vs DRAFT / other series
- L1/L3 day and session boundaries: RTH = 09:30-16:15 (settle print 16:14), ETH to 17:00, break 17:00-18:00, reopen 18:00; ORG anchors on the 16:14 settle, NDOG on 16:59 -> 18:00 (draft only had 17:00/18:00) (2025-b04/052@03:41; 2025-b03/010@04:58, @11:08). Note 2024-series also had NDOG = 16:59 to 18:00.
- L8 midnight open is a 30-minute range with high/low and SD projections, not a single price; also 9:30 open, 18:00 reopen, 17:00 and 16:14 settles to be charted (2025-b06/080@09:59; 2025-b02/006@73:50).
- L4 equal highs: the right-hand high is lower (buy-side) / the right-hand low higher (sell-side); "smooth" equal levels are expected to be made jagged; no numeric tolerance given (2025-b02/006@15:11; 2025-b05/056@12:59).
- L7 turtle soup: ICT sense is a raid + reversal on any TF inside narrative, with deferred entries; not the 20-day extreme (2025-b04/047@11:20; 2025-b01/003@24:15). A pool counts as taken only on a body close beyond (2025-b02/006@48:05).
- P1 FVG: include VI when drawing; ignore an indecisive candle that trades its whole range; merge several 1m gaps into the cleanest TF gap (2m, 10m) for the zone; first-presented FVG needs displacement (2025-b03/023@09:23; 2025-b03/030@14:06; 2025-b03/019@03:40; 2025-b06/075@08:21).
- P2: spelled "inversion"; bodies half-rule; "reclaimed FVG" distinct (resumes original role); ~80% of gaps formed into highs said to invert (claim) (2025-b03/012@08:52; 2025-b03/022@09:25).
- P6/P7/P9/P10: OB = series; CISD = open of the series; MT vs CE naming; bearish breaker uses the last down candle before the run higher; rejection block = candle close; propulsion = OB tested to its MT (see table). Difference to DRAFT: breaker is judged on bodies; mitigation block never mentioned.
- S1 MSS: no consistent rule: a through-trade suffices in one file; body close demanded in another; wick-break inside an opposing array is invalid (2025-b05/057@14:38; 2025-b03/020@03:10).
- S2 CISD: open of the delivering series (matches DRAFT), but wick-high variant and the "close of the Venom candle" variant exist (2025-b05/057@16:41; 2025-b04/051@15:07).

## MMXM material

### A. Market Maker Buy Model geometry (2025-b01/002, DXY daily, Nov 2025) - the only geometric statement in the series
Setup (all prices are examples; claimed scale-free, fractal, any TF):
1. Dealing range: top = the high that preceded the decline (the "original consolidation" / old opposing buy-side pool, best-case draw); bottom = the swing low that will be raided (2025-b01/002@27:33, @65:14, @98:02).
2. Smart money reversal (SMR) = the raid of that old sell-side low: a false break beneath an older low (turtle soup; the stops run are longs' stops plus breakout shorts) (2025-b01/002@66:36, @100:34).
3. The OPEN of the candle that made the raid is the pivot: the instant price trades back above it (one tick enough, not a close) a buy program is considered entered, and the bias flips from "premise" to bullish (2025-b01/002@101:04, @80:00, @81:31).
4. Low-hanging draw = 70.5% of the range measured high-to-low (equivalently the OTE level of the leg measured from the low); the first swing high above is an easier first target; the full return to the original consolidation is the best case (2025-b01/002@77:21, @98:02, @109:54).
5. Low-risk entry (invariant, "always"): the inversion FVG (a gap that was sold through, then reclaimed after the SMR) if one exists at the turn; if none, the FIRST bullish FVG that forms after the SMR (2025-b01/002@132:08, @138:51, @148:07).
6. 1st stage accumulation = the NEXT FVG after the low-risk entry, which must be anchored to an order block (OB + FVG pair); it carries price to the 70.5 level / nearest buy-side ("net buy side") (2025-b01/002@133:08, @133:40, @146:34, @147:35).
7. 2nd stage reaccumulation (called the "unicorn") = the FIRST retracement back into an inefficiency (FVG/BISI) after stage 1 has delivered; strongest, fastest leg, runs to the opposing liquidity. It is not stop-hunted: price may never trade back through the swing low formed at this stage; if it does the idea is wrong, exit (2025-b01/002@134:41, @141:55, @142:26, @33:58).
8. In-trade management along the model: initial stop under the SMR low; after a 3-bar swing low forms and rallies, stop to just under it; in stage 2 stop under its swing low. On any array bodies must stay in the upper half and may not close below CE (gap/wick) or MT (OB); first body close beyond CE = cut half and re-establish at the next array (2025-b01/002@70:10, @141:24, @116:36, @118:39, @125:25, @150:10).
9. The sell model is the exact mirror (high raid, 1st stage distribution, 2nd stage redistribution); a model need not look textbook, the mechanism must hold (2025-b01/002@28:03, @70:43, @138:21).
10. Worked example: daily DXY Oct-Nov 2025, raid low 96.377, raid candle open 96.617, 70.5 of the range matched an earlier high; inversion FVG called before it formed, CE hit, then BISI + OB + FVG (1st stage), retrace into an inefficiency, 2nd stage into the net buy side (2025-b01/002@32:26, @46:20, @102:06, @103:38, @133:40, @134:41).
Related scale-free spin-offs from the same lecture: Purge and Revert (after a swing low is run, draw = the high from three candles earlier, small run, fires often) and the "working-class hero" daily model (daily candle opening close above an old low; buy-stop at that low or enter on reclaim of the day's open; stop just under the raid; target 70.5 with first swing high as easier target; hourly version identical) (2025-b01/002@85:15, @85:46, @91:25, @44:15, @47:55, @63:01, @69:39, @70:10). Currency application: when DXY runs an old low, pairs with USD second are long scalps until that low is raided, then reverse; USD-first pairs mirror; choose the weaker/stronger pair by larger candles (2025-b01/002@47:55, @54:15, @152:13).

## Session-phase logic
- Sequence per day: pre-market / London (01:00-05:00) / Asia ranges leave pools; 07:00-10:00 is the window where daily setups form; the 09:30-10:00 opening range completes the frame; 10:00-11:00 Silver Bullet; lunch 11:30-13:30 pulls against the AM leg; PM OR 13:30-14:00; final-hour macros 14:50-15:10, 15:15-15:45, 15:50 close run (2025-b02/005@07:23; 2025-b04/034@29:38; 2025-b05/055@63:08).
- AM builds liquidity -> lunch macro retraces (consolidates / reverses / continues) -> PM uses both; repeated daily (2025-b04/034@29:38).
- Consolidation = no trade ("time distortion", troubled market): wait for the next protraction/leg and use arrays formed after it; the bigger the compression the bigger the release; HTF levels (CE of a key daily FVG) act as pivot while it lasts (2025-b02/006@136:22; 2025-b03/027@10:00, @12:03; 2025-b04/039@06:49).
- Expansion vs retracement/reversal: after a range day sit out; a rally day's AM leg ends at 10:00-11:00 and the first post-10:00 swing is the lunch target; at 09:30 the first drop on a bullish day is often a fake-out that traps breakout sellers (2025-b05/057@04:15; 2025-b03/022@12:26).
- Reversal test: draw reached, then stacked arrays flip (IFVG formed from the rally's arrays); after the weekly/daily objective is hit, reversal patterns are higher confidence (2025-b05/055@65:10; 2025-b05/057@17:41).
- Consolidation midpoint rule: after a bullish expansion pauses in a range, pullbacks that stay in the upper half keep bias; a close below the range equilibrium retires the morning long (2025-b01/001@41:40, @45:48).
- Minor buy/sell side = high/low of the first 30 min of RTH; if/then for the 10:00 Silver Bullet: hold above = run to HTF target; break then close back below = run the sell side and ORG upper quadrant becomes the objective; mid-range = do nothing (2025-b01/001@28:52, @33:24).
- Day types that change participation: FOMC (two-stage), CPI/PPI, NFP week, Monday/Friday (TGIF 20-40%), Thursday false highs, holiday and gap-risk weekends (reduce size), extremely volatile days (tariff spike) (see table).
- Bias discipline: one model, one instrument, not every day trades; use only the buy-side draws on a bearish week and only sell-side on a bullish week; occasional misses accepted (2025-b05/062@01:15; 2025-b04/051@16:41).
