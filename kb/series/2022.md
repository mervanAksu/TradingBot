# Series 2022 - digest (reduce of 2022-b01, 2022-b02)

**Coverage warning: only 11 of 41 episodes were available (episodes 2, 5, 9, 10, 14, 17, 19, 20, 23, 39, 41).**
Conclusions below are sample-based; absence of a topic here does not mean the series skips it.
Refs: `<batch>/<ep>@mm:ss`. Batch b01 = eps 2-23 (Jan-May 2022), b02 = eps 39, 41 (Jun 2022).
Scope: mostly NQ/ES intraday, some EURUSD/DXY. This is a basic "daily range + liquidity raid + FVG" series; MMXM is only implicit.

## Core teachings
1. Every trade names a draw on liquidity first (old pool or HTF FVG); no stated target = no trade (2022-b02/039@05:47; 2022-b01/019@59:50).
2. Entry chain: bias -> raid of a pool -> displacement through a 3-bar swing (MSS) -> retrace into the FVG left behind (or an OB inside it) (2022-b01/002@16:14, 2022-b01/002@29:03).
3. Order is fixed: raid, then structure break, then imbalance; exceeding an old high alone is not a signal (2022-b01/002@27:32).
4. Raid need only poke slightly beyond the level, but the reaction must be energetic; a lethargic reaction means pass (2022-b01/005@20:09, @21:12).
5. Do not chase after the break; the entry is the retrace (2022-b01/002@33:41).
6. Daily bias = direction of range expansion relative to the open, not the close; one contrary day does not cancel it (2022-b01/010@16:11, @22:24).
7. Power of 3 per day: open, fake (Judas) move against bias, then expansion; bullish buys below the open, bearish sells above (2022-b01/009@02:40, 2022-b01/010@25:38).
8. Two opens matter: 00:00 NY and 08:30 NY, first print of each (2022-b01/019@94:38). Midnight open is the day-direction anchor (2022-b02/039@10:22).
9. Opening-range projection: Judas leg size (open to high) projected on the other side of the open defines the band where setups are valid (2022-b01/010@13:38).
10. Targets: nearest opposing liquidity/imbalance, ideally short of the 50% of the range; pad exits in FX (2022-b01/002@47:34, 2022-b01/017@20:38).
11. Time-of-day framework for indices: AM 08:30-11:00, lunch 12:00-13:00 no new trades, PM from 13:30, close-flow 15:00-16:00 (2022-b01/005@09:58, 2022-b02/039@72:54, 2022-b02/041@04:47).
12. Reference swings: first obvious 3-bar high/low on 15m before 08:30 (AM) and the first pair after 13:30 (PM) (2022-b01/005@13:32, @31:02).
13. Big overnight run (about 02:00-05:00) = skip NY AM, resume after 13:00 on a raid of the pullback low (2022-b01/009@12:32).
14. Event days (FOMC etc): never trade the release; setup comes about 30 min later (2022-b01/023@01:38, @15:54).
15. Stand aside on sloppy charts, holidays, unstated bias/target (2022-b01/019@19:32, @64:27).
16. Risk: hard stop always; fixed fractional sizing; halve risk after a loss, restore after regaining about half (2022-b02/041@15:00, @22:14, @31:25).
17. Stop trail: at 50% of entry-to-target move stop to 25% of risk, at 75% to break-even (2022-b02/041@46:08).
18. FX: London killzone 02:00-05:00, NY FX killzone 07:00-10:00, DXY as barometer for EURUSD (2022-b01/020@07:45, @09:16; 2022-b01/017@10:24).

## Definitions vs DRAFT / other series
- Swing points: 3-candle only (DRAFT S6 had a fractal/ladder idea; keep 3-bar for STH/STL, intermediate needs more) (2022-b01/010@18:16).
- Day anchors: 00:00 NY and 08:30 NY opens, not the platform daily open; DRAFT L8 lists these as bias anchors - CONFIRMED, plus the "which one governs" rule above (2022-b01/019@94:38).
- DRAFT T1/CLAUDE.md windows: index AM here is 08:30-11:00 (new trades by about 10:45), lunch 12:00-13:00, PM 13:30; FX NY 07:00-10:00; London 02:00-05:00 (2022-b01/017@24:14; 2022-b01/020@07:45).
- Asia range: declared not applicable to index futures (differs from DRAFT T3 usage) (2022-b01/005@01:44); Asia low-movement for FX too (2022-b01/010@25:56).
- OB: last opposite-colour candle or run of consecutive ones before displacement leaving an FVG; use body; mean threshold 50% (DRAFT P6 agrees) (2022-b01/009@07:16; 2022-b01/019@155:18). Bearish OB zone may be the last up-close candle (2022-b01/017@15:31).
- Breaker (differs/refines DRAFT P7): buy-side pool run, then price breaks back below prior short-term low; zone = last down-close candle before the run; requires a known draw (2022-b02/039@20:13). Another episode describes bearish breaker as high-low-higher high pattern, stop just above it (2022-b01/010@40:00).
- OTE: this series uses 62-70% (sweet spot not 70.5) and 62% as an objective in places; DRAFT S4 is 62-79% (2022-b02/041@18:10; 2022-b01/019@109:00).
- "Intermediate-term high" = swing high carrying an FVG; invalidation for bias (2022-b01/017@14:29) - differs from DRAFT S6 (lower-STH-both-sides).
- Sweep vs run: sweep = brief beyond-and-return, run = through and continue (2022-b01/023@04:45). Matches DRAFT L7 only for sweep.
- Sweep of relative equal lows is not automatically a reversal (2022-b02/039@06:17) - contradicts a blanket Turtle Soup reading of L4/L7.
- Institutional FX levels: big figures ending 00/20/50/80 (new, 2022-b01/017@04:50).
- Not covered in sample: CISD, IFVG, BPR, mitigation block, NDOG/NWOG, SMT, IPDA ranges, SD projections.

## MMXM material
- The series presents only the sell/buy-side raid -> reversal -> run to opposite pool as a daily/session template, not the named MMXM phases (no SMR, no 1st/2nd distribution wording in sampled eps).
- Mini sell model: consolidation, fake break to trap, run of equal highs, MSS lower, FVG short, expansion into discount (2022-b01/002@20:49).
- Rationale: buy stops above highs supply the counterparty for large sellers (2022-b01/002@21:50; 2022-b01/019@174:29).
- Sell leg into HTF FVG, OB bounce midway, then reversal through previous-day high (2022-b01/023@19:50).
- Intra-day management maps to partial levels: first partial on break of first short-term low, then stop to better than BE, exit rest if cap candle is breached (2022-b02/039@61:12).
- Relevant to spec user-decision 4 (partials at re-distribution levels): the series gives fixed-step and percentage-of-risk trail, not level-based ones.

## Session-phase logic
- Day structure: AM move -> lunch consolidation (often a raid of the AM pool near 13:00) -> PM move; PM may continue or reverse AM; measured-move idea AM leg ~ PM leg (2022-b01/005@24:17, @29:26; 2022-b02/039@16:36).
- PM patterns only two: raid of a swing low then rally, or sudden displacement up then buy the dip (2022-b01/005@35:40).
- Close-flow acceleration around 15:40/15:50/16:00 NY (2022-b01/005@26:49); MOC macro 15:00-16:00 mentioned, undefined (2022-b02/039@72:54).
- One good AM trade = skip PM; after an AM loss/fail, PM re-attempt allowed (2022-b01/005@44:59; 2022-b02/039@72:54).
- Decide PM from what the daily range is doing: expanding, reversed, or waiting (2022-b02/039@15:36).
- Macros in the sense of xx:50-xx:10 are NOT taught in the sample; only 08:30, 09:30, 13:30, 14:00 (FOMC), 15:00-16:00 windows (conflicts with spec decision 3 emphasis; needs other series).
