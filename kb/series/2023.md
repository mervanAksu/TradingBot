## Core teachings
1. Price does only three things: run to liquidity (stops above highs / below lows), rebalance an inefficiency, or consolidate (2023-b05/050@07:46; 2023-b06/067@89:46; 2023-b07/098@39:52).
2. Bias is a prediction of one specific price (the draw on liquidity), asked in two steps: direction, then draw = a gap or a liquidity pool. Set it before the session; HTF fixes the draw, LTF only times the path (2023-b02/015@08:24; 2023-b07/075@05:28; 2023-b07/098@02:59).
3. "High probability" means the opposite case cannot be argued with the taught tools. If both sides can be framed, stand aside (2023-b03/025@13:45; 2023-b05/046@10:28).
4. Narrative is not bias: narrative = expected path (e.g. raid prior-day low first, then high; or stay inside prior range); bias = which side you trade (2023-b01/001@17:34).
5. Entry chain (the "2022 model" still used): pool raided -> displacement through a short-term swing -> retrace into an FVG / volume imbalance / OB inside the displacement leg, in the premium half for shorts (discount for longs). Do not chase the break (2023-b03/022@36:20, @57:15; 2023-b06/060@46:42).
6. PD arrays need narrative: an OB, breaker or IFVG is only valid when bias/draw agree with it (2023-b02/020@00:30; 2023-b07/085@39:50; 2023-b05/059@16:31).
7. Two 50% names: CE = midpoint of a gap, volume imbalance or wick; mean threshold (MT) = midpoint of an OB / breaker / propulsion block; equilibrium = midpoint of a dealing range (2023-b01/002@05:41; 2023-b02/012@04:26; 2023-b07/078@27:53).
8. Respect is judged on candle bodies/closes; wicks may overshoot ("mohawk", 0-2 ticks) (2023-b03/026@53:02, @48:54; 2023-b06/074@12:27; 2023-b07/078@58:08).
9. Opening prices are the day's premium/discount anchors: midnight NY (also 08:30, 09:30, 13:30 for sessions); shorts at/above, longs at/below (2023-b02/010@38:19; 2023-b04/043@15:08; 2023-b06/060@15:26).
10. Gaps are persistent magnets: NWOG (three prices, keep ~4-5 weeks), NDOG, and the RTH opening range gap (ORG). Price orbiting the NWOG = consolidating week; leaving it without return = expansion week (2023-b04/039@12:01; 2023-b04/030@10:52).
11. A gap is not "done" after one pass; every price should be offered both ways. A range traded both ways and left = balanced price range (BPR) (2023-b05/050@30:16; 2023-b03/026@65:21; 2023-b06/060@65:37).
12. Time windows matter more than candle shape: 09:30 sweep, 09:50-10:10 macro, Silver Bullet 10:00-11:00 and 14:00-15:00, lunch macro (10:50-11:10 / 11:50-12:10), 14:50-15:10, last hour 15:00-16:00 (see Session-phase logic).
13. Intermarket gate: DXY inverse to indices; ES/NQ/YM should agree, otherwise high-resistance / reduced size; SMT picks the stronger or the lagging instrument ("Sick Sister") (2023-b03/022@03:42; 2023-b05/046@49:06; 2023-b05/053@04:17).
14. Framework minimum: target in the 5-handle region (ES) but the setup should offer roughly 10 handles of room; first partial is the "bread and butter" (2023-b03/024@24:22; 2023-b05/046@33:10; later versions relax to 4-5 handles, 2023-b06/068@17:14).
15. Partials come first; the stop moves to break-even only after a partial; limit targets sit 0.5-1 pt (or 1 tick) before/beyond the reference extreme, never on the exact tick (2023-b02/010@58:44; 2023-b05/046@82:27; 2023-b05/050@54:56).
16. Scale-in at PD arrays (6/3/1, or 1/3/6 for breaker entries); do not pyramid once the move passes the 50% of the leg or nears target (2023-b02/021@19:57; 2023-b03/022@49:36; 2023-b06/070@36:26).
17. Stand aside on news: CPI/NFP/FOMC release bars, FOMC until 14:45, two news drivers in one session, NFP-week Thu/Fri, holiday-shortened days, August (2023-b02/021@05:39; 2023-b04/028@03:21; 2023-b08/100@51:24; 2023-b07/081@00:50).
18. Time/progress filter: after entry, price should show follow-through within a few minutes (2-3 min in chop, ~10 min on 1m); no movement = wrong/early, close (2023-b03/022@103:14; 2023-b03/025@17:19; 2023-b03/027@24:53).
19. TGIF: after a one-directional week, Friday retraces ~20-30% of the weekly range; target price can be computed in advance (2023-b06/074@28:13; 2023-b07/085@17:12; 2023-b07/089@00:48).
20. Thursday often prints the opposite extreme of the week once targets are met; weekly power of 3 anchored on the Monday midnight open (2023-b08/100@08:24; 2023-b06/072@03:28; 2023-b06/060@72:23).
21. Not taught as signals: DOM / footprint / order-book data (explicitly rejected), candle patterns, indicators (2023-b07/075@00:48; 2023-b02/019@10:30).

## Definitions vs DRAFT / other series
- Day boundary / open (L8): 00:00 NY price is the day anchor; 18:00 open only for NDOG/NWOG (2023-b01/006@22:38).
- MSS (S1): trade-through (even one tick) suffices; DRAFT demanded a close beyond. CISD (S2) is used as "OB confirms when its OPEN is crossed" (2023-b02/012@30:08; 2023-b07/078@53:32).
- FVG naming (P1): BISI = bullish FVG (up-close middle), SIBI = bearish; mid prices differ by object (CE vs MT vs equilibrium) (2023-b07/078@13:36, @27:53).
- Liquidity void (P11): stricter than DRAFT: no prints at all; volume imbalance (P4) is body-to-body gap with wick overlap; they are different arrays (2023-b02/019@13:02).
- OB (P6): bullish OB may be a 2-candle series; the zone high is touched, MT 50%; only valid if the narrative agrees and a displacement/OPEN-cross follows (2023-b08/100@17:04; 2023-b03/022@12:57).
- Breaker (P7): defined by the 3-swing sequence with bodies, not by "OB that failed"; mitigation block (P8) = same without taking liquidity, weaker (2023-b06/060@59:34).
- Propulsion block (P10): candle that trades into an earlier OB and respects its 50%, not just "propels" (2023-b02/019@10:00).
- NWOG/NDOG (P5): precise 16:59/18:00 prints; RTH variant and ORG added; CE plus 25/75 quarters; persistence (NWOG months, ORG 3-5 days) (2023-b04/039; 2023-b06/068@09:29).
- Killzones (T1): FX NY 07:00-09:00 / London 02:00-05:00 / London close 10:00-12:00 stated once; index work uses 08:30-11:00 AM and 13:30/14:00-16:00 PM windows (2023-b01/008@01:06; 2023-b01/001@48:02).
- Session ranges (L3): PM 13:30-16:00, lunch 12:00-13:30 (DRAFT used other windows) (2023-b06/067).
- OTE (S4): appears only rarely (Sick Sister in OTE 62-79%); SD projections replace Fibonacci retracement tools (2023-b05/053@08:24; 2023-b04/029@45:22).
- SMT (L9): extended to Sick Sister / sixth sister; also YM and DXY/EUR/GBP intermarket reads (2023-b04/030@49:20).
- Turtle soup (L7): an entry with HTF bias, not only a target (2023-b06/070).

## MMXM material
- Buy-model walk stated verbally: original consolidation -> sell-side run (smart money reversal) -> low-risk buy (OB/FVG/breaker) -> re-accumulation -> back to the original consolidation area / BSL; every stage is a possible entry; sell model mirrors with accumulation, re-accumulation, reversal, low-risk sell, distribution, redistribution, second-stage distribution (2023-b01/003@88:12; 2023-b02/015@14:32; 2023-b06/072@30:08).
- Daily skeleton: consolidation (Asia/London/pre-open) -> manipulation (Judas) beyond midnight/9:30 open against the intended side -> sweep of opposite-side pool -> shift + FVG -> expansion to the stated draw, with secondary entries at each new FVG/OB (2023-b01/009@03:44; 2023-b02/013@08:30; 2023-b06/069@15:26).
- Continuation vs retracement: FVG left open on departure ("breakaway") = continuation; price returning into the FVG and filling = retrace; measuring gap about half-way confirms the extent (2023-b01/003@29:26; 2023-b01/001@41:16).
- Targets/partials: each re-distribution level (new short-term high, relative equal highs, CE of daily wick/NWOG) gets a partial; "run down equity" protocol: one contract off per new extreme; main target = HTF draw (prior close, NWOG edge/CE, daily FVG, volume imbalance, SD confluence) (2023-b01/003@64:46; 2023-b02/020@04:40; 2023-b03/027@38:56).
- Stops: 1 tick beyond the entry array / MT; line in the sand = HTF gap or MT; invalidation stated before entry (2023-b01/002@08:18; 2023-b02/012@28:03).
- Reversal vs retracement day check: if the HTF objective is reached by noon, PM model aims back at lunch-hour lows via FVG at the old high -> CE rejection -> MSS -> retest short (2023-b02/015@30:51; 2023-b05/059@43:15).
- Buy/sell program concept: algorithmic repricing, not a classic trend; do not pick tops inside it; retail flags are the trap run first (2023-b07/076@05:18).
- Advanced breaker as an MMXM stage: A-to-B leg, second stage of liquidity below the first low, enter inside leg (2023-b06/070@33:22).
- Examples with dates/instruments: ES 2023-01-17 buy -> 4030 (2023-b02/017@03:11); NQ 2023-01-04 long to 11048 (2023-b01/003@17:02); ES 2023-02-01 FOMC sweep -> 4112 (2023-b02/021@40:18); ES 2023-05-22 evolving Silver Bullet short to NWOG CE (2023-b05/057@19:25); NQ 2023-07-14 TGIF short (2023-b07/075@99:48).
- Not covered here: explicit phase labels (SMR, 1st/2nd distribution) as a state machine; the notes give the stage sequence only in prose.

## Session-phase logic
- Pre-09:30: consolidation/expansion in Asia/London; if the "easy" move has already run to sell-side before 09:30, expect 50/50 chop at the open and wait for PM (2023-b03/022@75:38).
- 08:30 data = trigger/judas; 09:30-10:00 opening range gets little weight; then 09:50-10:10 macro and Silver Bullet (2023-b04/029@12:03; 2023-b05/045@13:45).
- Lunch 12:00-13:30: consolidation/time distortion; 10:50-11:10 and 11:50-12:10 macros run to AM stops; noon is the day midpoint and a reversal point once the daily draw is met (2023-b05/046@147:16; 2023-b05/059@28:00; 2023-b06/068@12:34).
- PM: manipulation 13:30-13:45 hits AM stops, 14:00 preferred entry hour (13:15-13:30 too early), 14:50-15:10 macro, final run 15:15-15:50 into pool; close usually just off the high on non-trend days (2023-b02/019@20:42; 2023-b03/027@28:57).
- Day types: trend/expansion vs consolidation read from NWOG orbit, prior inside day (large range next), outside day (lean toward untouched side), seek-and-destroy profile (leave after first run) (2023-b02/012@16:15; 2023-b07/081@01:22; 2023-b03/024@13:44).
- Floor profile: AM move, lunch consolidation or reversal, PM repeat in half the time (2023-b06/067@38:45).
- Week: Mon-Wed morning best in news weeks; Thursday opposite extreme; Friday TGIF retrace 20-30% (2023-b01/003@02:33; 2023-b08/100@08:24).
